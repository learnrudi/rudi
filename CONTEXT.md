# LearnRUDI Context

This glossary defines product and offer language for LearnRUDI pages. It exists to keep public copy, docs, and funnel decisions from collapsing distinct RUDI offers into the same name.

## Language

**Live AI Workflow Clinic**:
A public, workflow-centered RUDI clinic where participants bring a messy workflow and leave with a clearer working structure. This is the canonical name for the general $97 public clinic offer.
_Avoid_: Camp Claude, webinar, course

**Camp Claude**:
A separate Claude-specific RUDI series for applying Claude to workflows. It should not be used as the name for the general live workflow clinic or broader B2B funnel.
_Avoid_: Generic AI clinic, general workflow clinic

**Two-Door Offer Architecture**:
RUDI's acquisition model has two parallel entry paths: a free curated convening for named people inside the trust radius, and a paid public clinic for cold or lower-context buyers. These paths are not sequential, and a buyer does not need to attend the public clinic before qualifying for private team or org work.
_Avoid_: Ladder, ascension funnel, staircase

**Curated Convening**:
A free, invite-only working room for named operators, org leaders, referral partners, and one relevant teammate when useful. It is a relationship and diagnosis room, not a public broadcast or list-building webinar.
_Avoid_: Open webinar, free public viewing, applicant funnel

**Public Team Pass**:
A $297 public clinic pass for up to three people from the same organization. It should preserve or slightly raise the per-seat value relative to the $97 individual seat rather than discount intent.
_Avoid_: $250 team pass, group discount
