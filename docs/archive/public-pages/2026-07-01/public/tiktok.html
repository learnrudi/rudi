RUDI TikTok Integration

RUDI helps creators prepare educational short-form videos, review captions, and upload approved videos to a connected TikTok account.

What the integration does

The TikTok integration is designed for creators and teams who already make educational AI training videos in RUDI. A user selects a finished video, reviews the caption, connects their TikTok account, and asks RUDI to send that video to TikTok.

The first production target is upload-to-draft behavior. That means RUDI can send the selected video to TikTok, and the creator completes the final review inside TikTok before the video goes live.

User flow

  1. The creator signs in to RUDI and chooses a finalized vertical video.
  2. The creator reviews the title, caption, hashtags, and platform readiness checks.
  3. The creator connects TikTok through TikTok OAuth and grants the requested scopes.
  4. RUDI uploads only the selected video and caption metadata to TikTok.
  5. The creator completes final review and posting inside TikTok.
  6. RUDI stores the TikTok post URL or upload status in the creator's publishing log when provided.

Data handled

RUDI handles only the TikTok data needed to complete the creator's requested publishing action.

  • Basic TikTok account identity returned by OAuth, such as account ID and display information.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, stored server-side and encrypted.
  • The specific video file, caption, hashtags, and upload status selected by the creator.
  • Publishing logs used to show whether a platform upload succeeded or failed.

RUDI does not sell TikTok account data or use it for advertising profiles.

App description

RUDI helps creators prepare educational AI videos and upload approved drafts to their connected TikTok account.

Scope explanation

user.info.basic identifies the connected TikTok account. video.upload uploads a creator-selected video to TikTok for final review. RUDI should request video.publish only when direct publishing is implemented and ready for review.
