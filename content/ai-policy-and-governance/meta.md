## Editorial scope

The perspective begins with two questions: what capability or activity is being regulated, and which legislative domain is being evaluated. Four overlapping technology areas precede six legislative domains. National security is treated through capabilities, competition, and components. The examples distinguish enacted law, introduced proposals, agency research, voluntary arrangements, and a treaty framework.

The article uses “legislators” for the general audience. National security is described as a major domain, without an unsupported ranking as the largest. Diffusion models are identified as a subset of generative methods. The domain map and recommendations are RUDI analysis, not an official legal taxonomy.
