Microsoft 365 Copilot

Put Copilot to work with your team.

The environment

Start with the Microsoft tools your team uses.

Microsoft 365 Copilot brings AI assistance into work with tools such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams. Copilot Studio supports building agents. We begin by checking the products, licenses, and access your team actually has.

Copilot Chat, Microsoft 365 Copilot, and Copilot Studio have different capabilities and licensing arrangements. We confirm the features available to participants before choosing practice tasks or scoping an implementation.

How RUDI helps

Choose the support your team needs.

Team training

Practice on relevant work: prepare a meeting summary, develop a document draft, or organize information for a decision. Participants learn to give clear instructions, check sources, and review the result. Workshop length, group size, and follow-up support are agreed in the proposal.

Readiness and workspace support

Identify useful starting tasks and the information they depend on. Work with your Microsoft administrator on account access, approved data, sharing permissions, and practical employee guidance. Define who approves changes and supports users.

Workflow implementation

Map one recurring process, agree on the permitted systems and actions, and build or configure a scoped pilot. Where an agent is appropriate, evaluate Copilot Studio and the connections the task requires. Test review steps and exceptions before rollout.

An example to scope with your team

Turn meeting notes into a reviewed project update.

A team lead wants a useful weekly update from approved meeting notes and project information. The pilot can begin with a draft that the team lead checks before sharing.

  1. 01Approved source

    Use the agreed notes and project documents.

  2. 02Copilot-assisted draft

    Prepare a summary and proposed action list.

  3. 03Team lead review

    Check owners, dates, sources, and missing decisions.

  4. 04Approved update

    Share through the agreed channel.

Copilot training can sit alongside consulting, custom solutions, and integrations involving Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, Dynamics 365 Business Central, Dynamics 365 Enterprise, and SQL. We confirm the systems, access, dependencies, and delivery responsibilities in the proposal.

Responsible AI

Keep a person responsible for the work.

Agree on approved information, access, review, and disclosure before the pilot begins. For an agent that can take actions, define what needs approval, where it must stop, and who resolves exceptions. Configure those limits in the tools and workflow.

RUDI’s RESPECT framework covers responsible ownership, equitable access, safety, privacy, efficiency and effectiveness, control, and transparency. Evaluate the result with the people who will use it.

Ongoing support can include accessible practice, office hours, workspace administration, or operation of a managed digital worker. The proposal defines the responsibilities and schedule.
