RUDI/Greater Cincinnati/AI Readiness Index

RUDI Research · In development

The Greater Cincinnati AI Readiness Index.

How are organizations across Greater Cincinnati preparing to use AI? RUDI is developing a regional research initiative to examine that question and identify where leaders and institutions can help.

Current status

Research design and partnership development.

No findings have been published yet. RUDI is inviting regional organizations and partners to help shape the first edition.

Discuss research participationExplore the regional hub →

The research question

How ready are Greater Cincinnati organizations for AI?

The proposed study would examine how organizations choose AI projects, prepare employees, manage data and tools, and oversee the work. These six areas would help identify what supports adoption and where organizations need help.

Strategy and decisions
How do leaders understand current AI use, choose priorities, allocate investment, and assign responsibility?
Capability and adoption
What are employees using, where do learning needs differ by role, and what helps people keep practicing?
Workflows and value
Where is AI changing tasks and processes, which uses create value, and where is human judgment essential?
Technology and data
How do tools, access permissions, information sources, integrations, and technical constraints shape use?
Governance and responsible use
How do organizations handle policies, privacy, verification, oversight, vendor risk, accountability, and incidents?
Regional conditions
Which shared resources, partnerships, talent, research, and programs could help organizations prepare?

Proposed evidence model

Surveys, interviews, and readiness assessments.

The research design proposes combining a broad organizational survey with executive interviews and a smaller set of detailed assessments.

Proposed sources of evidence for the first edition
MethodWhat it would examine
Organizational surveyCurrent AI activity, workforce capability, governance, adoption, and leadership priorities across regional organizations.
Executive interviewsDecisions, barriers, use cases, organizational dynamics, and emerging needs in leaders’ own accounts.
Readiness assessmentsA smaller set of detailed organizational assessments and engagement evidence to test and enrich the regional patterns.

Intended outputs

Reports and a regional discussion.

These are planned outputs. The first edition is still in development.

Annual report
An account of regional readiness, strengths, gaps, and priority actions.
Executive brief
A concise view of the findings and priorities for organizational leaders.
Sector views
Analysis of differences across sectors or organization types, where sample size and evidence support it.
Regional convening
A working conversation about the findings and possible partnerships, programs, and next steps.
