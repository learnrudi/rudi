What does your team need help doing?

Tell us who you’re supporting, what tools you use, and what work you want to improve.

What this is

A conversation about your current work and next decisions.

We’ll discuss the decisions you face, what is already happening, and whether RUDI is a useful fit.

A useful first conversation

Bring the situation as it is.

You do not need a finished AI strategy or a fully defined project. It is enough to describe the questions, activity, pressure, or opportunity that prompted the conversation.

  • What leadership is trying to decide
  • What employees or teams are already doing
  • What work or workflows may need to change
  • Where governance, capability, or technology is unclear
  • What a useful next step would enable

Possible next steps

The first recommendation should fit the real need.

We’ll discuss whether your team needs training, a department workflow program, a tool built for a specific task, or help with its AI environment. A readiness assessment is available when you need a broader starting point.

01

Clarify

Define the decisions, stakeholders, current state, constraints, and desired outcome.

02

Diagnose

Identify the skills, process, tool or support that would help your team.

03

Recommend

Recommend a next step based on the decisions, people, and workflows involved.
