Begin with a readiness conversation.

Tell us what your organization is trying to understand, change, or implement. RUDI will help identify the most useful starting point.

What this is

A focused first conversation—not a generic sales call.

We’ll discuss the decisions you face, what is already happening, and whether RUDI is a useful fit.

A useful first conversation

Bring the situation as it is.

You do not need a finished AI strategy or a fully defined project. It is enough to describe the questions, activity, pressure, or opportunity that prompted the conversation.

  • What leadership is trying to decide
  • What employees or teams are already doing
  • What work or workflows may need to change
  • Where governance, capability, or technology is unclear
  • What a useful next step would enable

Possible next steps

The first recommendation should fit the real need.

After the conversation, RUDI may recommend a readiness assessment, a focused leadership session, a scoped strategy engagement, a workforce program, an adoption intervention, an implementation discovery—or no engagement yet.
