The leadership problem

AI adoption creates organizational questions before it creates technology answers.

Employees are already experimenting. Vendors are making promises. Leaders need a way to separate useful opportunity from avoidable risk—and turn scattered activity into deliberate organizational capability.

The engagement model

How RUDI works.

The exact scope changes by organization. The underlying discipline does not: establish the baseline, make choices, build capability, change work, and measure what happens.

01 · Understand

Establish the baseline.

Interviews, surveys, artifacts, workflow observation, and readiness analysis reveal the current state.

02 · Prioritize

Choose where to focus.

Align leaders, evaluate use cases, define principles, and sequence a practical roadmap.

03 · Enable

Build the conditions.

Develop workforce capability, governance, tools, champions, and workflow support.

04 · Implement

Change the work.

Pilot, deploy, support adoption, evaluate outcomes, and scale what proves useful.
