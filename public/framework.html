The RUDI Readiness Framework

01 / Understand your starting point

Assess how your people use AI.

Look at their current tasks, skills, and data-handling practices. Learn your organization’s familiarity and comfort with generative AI.

The tech-savvy organization

Highly fluent in technology, yet unable to figure out how to incorporate AI strategically. The tools are there, but adoption has plateaued.

The organization starting from scratch

Little to no AI experience, unsure where to begin. Needs a clear roadmap but doesn’t want to guess.

Workflows
How people use AI in daily tasks: drafting, analyzing, and automating.
Data
What data is safe to use, with an understanding of boundaries and compliance.
Culture
Building confidence and adoption across the organization.

From surveying hundreds of organizations, from educators to Fortune 500 companies, we’ve learned the real ROI comes from people’s efficiency at the micro level: using AI to draft documents, analyze data, automate repetitive tasks, and build micro-tools within the organization.

People and culture

The cultural axis

  • Reduces fear and demystifies AI
  • Embeds tools into daily workflows
  • Builds literacy through learning

Focus: How people learn and adopt AI responsibly.

Tools and systems

The technical axis

  • Aligns tools, data, and infrastructure
  • Ensures compliance and ethics
  • Modernizes metrics and ROI

Focus: Infrastructure and system optimization.

RUDI integrates both approaches. We build comfort and literacy first, then layer in the governance and infrastructure, addressing the cultural and technical dimensions of AI adoption.

02 / The literacy framework

Three dimensions of AI competency.

Every skill is measured across these three foundational areas. Together, they connect understanding, practical use, and responsible oversight.

01

Conceptual understanding

Understanding AI at a high level: how it works, why outputs vary, its limitations, and its societal impact.

02

Operational skills

Using AI tools in practice: writing effective prompts, building workflows, and integrating AI into daily work.

03

Governance & ethics

Oversight, ethics, and responsible use: understanding risks, applying policies, and leading safe AI adoption.

3 dimensions ×5 levels =15 competency points

03 / The proficiency scale

Five levels of mastery.

Apply the same scale across all three dimensions to understand current capability and plan the next stage of learning.

  1. 0

    No Exposure

    Conceptual
    Never heard of generative AI
    Operational
    Never opened an AI tool
    Governance
    No awareness of AI risks
  2. 1

    Pre-Beginner

    Conceptual
    Heard "AI" or "ChatGPT" but can't explain
    Operational
    Can open tools but unsure what to type
    Governance
    Knows AI may be biased but not how
  3. 2

    Beginner

    Conceptual
    Can describe AI as "a tool that generates text/images"
    Operational
    Can try basic prompts, recognize when output is off
    Governance
    Can name risks like privacy, misinformation, bias
  4. 3

    Intermediate

    Conceptual
    Understands training data, bias, why outputs vary
    Operational
    Uses AI for work tasks with cross-checking
    Governance
    Knows policies, applies them correctly
  5. 4

    Advanced

    Conceptual
    Can critically discuss AI's societal role, ethics, limitations
    Operational
    Designs AI workflows, mentors peers
    Governance
    Leads conversations on AI policy, ethics, adoption

04 / Put the framework into practice

Build habits for responsible AI use.

What data can I use? How carefully should I check outputs? What questions should I ask? These models connect the readiness journey to daily practice.

Your journey

The Pyramid

The emotional arc from fear to confidence. Familiarity and comfort provide the foundation for literacy and fluency.

“Where am I going?”

Your measurement

The 3×5 Matrix

Systematic assessment across 15 competency points, giving structure to your learning and progress.

“Where am I now?”

Your practice

The 3D Model

Daily habits for responsible AI use, with questions to ask and levels of review to apply.

“How do I use AI safely?”

Habits of responsible use

The questions you ask

Source tracing
Where did this come from?
Cross-referencing
Does this hold up elsewhere?
Perspective testing
Whose voice is present or missing?
Boundary marking
What are the limits?
Bias awareness
What assumptions shape this?

Levels of scrutiny

How carefully to check

  1. 01

    Light touch

    Low-stakes tasks and internal brainstorming.

  2. 02

    Standard review

    Client-facing content and published work.

  3. 03

    Deep verification

    Legal, compliance, and high-stakes decisions.
