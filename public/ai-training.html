What changed

AI training should start with the work, not the tool.

Most teams do not need another tour of features. They need to understand where AI belongs in their day, what context it needs, what output is useful, and what judgment should stay human.

Operator

Use AI for clear tasks: drafting, summarizing, analysis, research, and review.

Designer

Structure the work so AI has the right goal, context, constraints, examples, and review criteria.

Builder

Turn repeated tasks into reusable workflows, templates, automations, or agent-ready systems.

How sessions work

The repeatable flow stays simple.

01

Bring a real problem

Prompts, documents, decisions, workflows, content, research, email, planning, or operational friction.

02

Define the work

Clarify the goal, audience, context, constraints, source material, and decision points.

03

Rebuild the workflow

Turn the messy task into a prompt, template, checklist, or reusable AI-assisted process.

04

Decide what repeats

Identify what should become a template, what should be automated, and what needs human judgment.
