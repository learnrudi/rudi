Work with one team

Learn while improving a real process.

Your team brings the work, the systems, and the questions. RUDI helps map the process, teaches relevant AI skills, and identifies what to improve. When a new tool is useful, we can build and test it with the people who will use it.

Start with a recurring task such as preparing a weekly report, checking incoming documents, or handling internal requests.

The engagement

Map the work. Practice with AI. Build and test.

Map the work

Document inputs, handoffs, repetitive steps and decisions. Establish what good output looks like and how long the work takes today.

Practice with AI

Use approved examples from the team’s work. Teach people how to provide context, check outputs and decide when to ask for help.

Build and test

Select a feasible improvement together. Configure a reusable workflow, build an agent or automation, or develop a small application when the process needs one.

Support the team

Name the human owner, document review and stop conditions, and compare quality and total effort with the starting point. Use RESPECT to check access, safety, privacy, control, and disclosure before expanding the workflow.

What you leave with

A process your team understands and can use.

A documented workflow

A shared map of the task, the information it needs, and the decisions people retain.

Practical team skills

Guided practice and reusable instructions for the people doing the work.

An agreed implementation

A scoped improvement with acceptance checks. Access, integrations and complexity determine what we build and the delivery schedule.

A basis for the next decision

Compare results with the starting point and decide what to support, adjust or expand.
