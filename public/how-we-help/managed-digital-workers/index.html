RUDI/How We Help/Managed Digital Workers

Managed AI Service

Digital workers operated and maintained by RUDI.

RUDI builds and operates AI agents for recurring work. Every digital worker has a named human manager, approved system access, defined procedures, and review requirements matched to the work.

Discuss a workflowMeet the first worker

In plain English

Give a digital worker a defined job and clear boundaries.

RUDI sets up each digital worker with a role, an identity, approved access, a work schedule, and a definition of done. We monitor its work and route exceptions to the people responsible for reviewing them.

01

A digital worker

One AI agent with a named role and digital identity. It follows approved procedures, works inside approved systems, completes routine cases, and stops when human judgment is required.

02

A digital workforce

Several digital workers coordinated across connected jobs such as intake, payables, reconciliation, reporting, and follow-up.

03

A digital workforce manager

RUDI defines, trains, connects, tests, operates, monitors, governs, and improves the workforce. Your team sets the business rules and retains required approvals.

Best-fit work

Identify frequent tasks that take substantial staff time.

The strongest managed-workforce opportunities happen frequently and consume meaningful human time every time they occur.

↑More occurrences
Frequent + briefSimple automation

Useful, but a lighter managed load.

Best managed-workforce targetMany occurrences + lots of time

Large, recurring labor load.

Occasional + briefLower priority

Limited recurring value.

Occasional + time-heavyProject support

Valuable, but not continuous work.

Little timeHuman time per occurrence →Lots of time

Then add the third dimension: cost, error risk, deadline pressure, and the opportunity cost of pulling people away from higher-value work.

Design the job

Define the worker like you would define a role.

The worker needs a job, an identity, approved access, an activity level, human oversight, and a clear definition of done.

01

Role + outcome

What work does this worker own?Named role · definition of done
02

Systems + permissions

Where can it work, and what can it do?Read · prepare · write · approve
03

Activity level

How often must it watch or act?Continuous · hourly · daily · weekly
04

Human oversight

When must it stop and ask?Exceptions · approvals · response deadline
05

Reporting + analysis

What should it show, and when?Per event · weekly · monthly

Activity, volume, coverage, integrations, permissions, and response time shape the service scope and price.
