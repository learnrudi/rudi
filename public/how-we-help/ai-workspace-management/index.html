Setup and support

Give your team an AI environment they can use.

Get help configuring AI workspaces, permissions, and connectors. Establish practical policies and ongoing support as your team’s use grows.

We scope the work around your selected platforms, existing administrators and data rules. Employee workspaces and developer or API environments have different needs; we clarify which are part of the engagement.

What we help manage

The settings and practices behind everyday AI use.

Workspace and account setup

Organize users, administrative roles, access and onboarding. Help your existing administrators document who can use which tools.

Connectors and shared context

Configure approved connections and reusable resources. Check what information is accessible and what actions each workflow can perform.

AI policy and practical guidance

Help draft or improve your organization’s AI-use guidance, then connect it to examples, review responsibilities and employee training.

Usage, cost and ongoing support

Review usage and available cost information, resolve agreed support needs, and revisit settings and workflows as your team’s needs change.

Choose the support you need

A defined setup project or ongoing management.

Initial setup or review

Assess the current configuration, agree on changes, make the approved updates and hand over documentation.

Ongoing support

Agree on supported platforms, administrative responsibilities, review frequency and response expectations. The scope determines the service.
