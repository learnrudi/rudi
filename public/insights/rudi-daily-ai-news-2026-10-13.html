AI News for October 13, 2026

Google launched Gemini 4 Argon, Meta released Muse Wonder, and NVIDIA prepared RTX Spark laptops for a local-AI push. Intel introduced its 480GB D270 inference chip as policymakers and publishers argued over who gets to test and profit from frontier models. All 64 stories from the day, below.

October 13, 202664 stories · 9 categories

Google publicly launched Gemini 4 Argon worldwide, making its first Gemini 4 model available to AI Pro and Ultra subscribers and offering introductory API pricing below rivals. Meta released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full for agents and claimed the models can compete with OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra and Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.1. NVIDIA prepared RTX Spark laptops for October 16 availability with new local models, a personal AI router, Windows tooling, creator updates, and faster gaming software. The model contest was moving onto subscriptions, agent apps, and local machines at once.

Intel unveiled the D270 inference GPU with up to 480GB of LPDDR5X memory, a 350-watt design, and volume shipping described as likely in 2027. Nvidia, AMD, Google, and Meta backed a common data-center specification covering physical layout, cooling, and power architecture. Meanwhile, more than 80 publishers rallied behind a standard for tracking and monetizing AI use of news, while the White House restored UK access to unreleased frontier models for pre-release testing after a reported restriction, according to UK AI minister Kanishka Narayan.

Anthropic says new safeguards reduced extraction of Claude’s reasoning data by 90–99%, even as they forced the company to reject paying traffic; Reuters also reports that Anthropic and OpenAI sought approval rights over some evaluator disclosures. Denmark proposed banning realistic AI deepfakes shared without consent, with exceptions for satire and parody. Researchers introduced a six-campaign benchmark for agents conducting real wet-lab experiments, while Workday said its Sana agents would operate inside tools including Claude, Copilot, Slack, and Teams under company permissions, policies, and approvals.

What People Are Asking

What is Gemini 4 Argon, and who can use it?

Google publicly launched Gemini 4 Argon globally as its first Gemini 4 model. It is available to AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, with introductory API pricing described as lower than rivals and new agentic coding and task features in the Gemini app. Mashable →

What are Meta’s Muse Wonder models?

Meta released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full as frontier models built for agents and trained on its Prometheus data center. Meta claims they can compete with OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra and Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.1. KuCoin →

When do NVIDIA RTX Spark laptops launch?

RTX Spark laptop availability is scheduled for October 16. NVIDIA is pairing the launch with Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B, the PAIR personal AI router, Llama for Windows, creator-tool updates, and a faster DLSS 5 model. gadgetpilipinas.net →

What is Intel’s D270 AI chip?

Intel’s D270 is an AI inference GPU offering up to 480GB of LPDDR5X memory at 350 watts. Intel is positioning it as a less expensive alternative amid HBM supply constraints, with volume shipping described as likely in 2027. Startup Fortune →

Did the White House restore UK access to frontier AI models?

Yes. The White House reversed a reported restriction and restored access to unreleased frontier models for pre-release testing, including Google DeepMind’s Gemini 4 Argon, according to UK AI minister Kanishka Narayan. shattered.io →

Is Denmark banning AI deepfakes without consent?

Not yet. Denmark is proposing legislation that would prohibit sharing realistic AI-generated depictions of a person’s face, voice, or body without consent, while preserving exceptions for satire and parody. whatsthebigdata.com →

Why is Anthropic turning away some paying AI traffic?

Anthropic says anti-distillation safeguards reduced extraction of Claude reasoning data by 90–99%, at the cost of rejecting some paying traffic. Reuters also reports that Anthropic and OpenAI sought approval rights over what independent evaluators may publish. The Deep Dive →

Can Workday’s Sana agents run inside Claude and Microsoft Copilot?

Yes, according to Workday. The company says its Sana orchestration engine will operate inside Claude, Gemini Enterprise, Microsoft Copilot, Teams, and Slack while applying company permissions, policies, and approvals to HR and finance work. Workday →
