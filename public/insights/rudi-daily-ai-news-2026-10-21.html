AI News for October 21, 2026

NVIDIA claims DSX can cut AI data-center power use by up to 30%, Nemotron 3.5 puts a 74B model within a 24GB PC memory budget, and ElevenLabs is trading transcript access and component control for a managed real-time voice stack. An Irish survey, meanwhile, finds most HR leaders expect job redesign rather than large workforce cuts. All 28 stories from the day, below.

October 21, 202628 stories · 6 categories

NVIDIA says its DSX MaxLPS platform can cut power consumption by up to 30% while increasing inference throughput within a fixed power budget. That efficiency claim lands amid a physical buildout: DP World is expanding its specialized U.S. data-center logistics network to nearly 50 sites, while ASI raised $325 million at a reported $3 billion valuation to expand simulation AI across aviation, defense, and energy. Power, freight, security, and inventory are becoming as central to AI competition as the models themselves.

Nvidia released its Nemotron 3.5 open-model lineup, including a 74B Bolt model designed to run locally in 24GB of memory. ElevenLabs launched speech-to-speech models with sub-second latency, but its managed service withholds transcripts and component-level control. Elsewhere, Zendesk introduced agents for multi-step IT workflows, and Microsoft's TypeScript 7.1 beta added new import syntax support. The products differ, but each asks users to choose where execution happens and how much control they retain.

Only 4% of 325 Irish HR leaders surveyed expect AI to significantly reduce employee numbers; most anticipate redesigned roles instead. Public sentiment is less settled: an Ipsos study across 32 countries found excitement and nervousness about AI near parity. Those expectations are forming alongside immediate risks, from AI-manipulated campaign images, cloned voices, and deepfake audio to AI-related regulatory, cybersecurity, and military scrutiny. People may disagree about the destination; institutions are already rewriting the route.

What People Are Asking

How much power can NVIDIA DSX save?

NVIDIA claims its DSX MaxLPS platform can use up to 30% less power and deliver 49% more inference throughput within a fixed power budget. Nscale tested the system in Iceland, while CoreWeave and Google Cloud are evaluating adoption. 24/7 Wall St. →

Can Nvidia Nemotron 3.5 run on a PC?

One report says the 74B Nemotron 3.5 Bolt model is designed to run locally with 24GB of memory. The lineup also includes a 120B-parameter Super VL enterprise model. shattered.io →

What is ElevenLabs Realtime?

ElevenLabs launched Eleven Realtime, a family of speech-to-speech voice-agent models combining sub-second latency with high spoken-reasoning accuracy. The managed service does not provide transcript access or component-level control. Tech Times →

Do HR leaders expect AI to cause major job cuts?

Not according to most respondents in one Ibec survey of 325 Irish HR leaders. Only 4% expect significant employee reductions, while most anticipate that AI will redesign roles instead. The Irish Times →

Are people more excited or worried about AI?

The 2026 Ipsos AI Monitor found excitement and nervousness near parity across 32 countries. It also found regional differences, expectations of greater future impact, reported workplace time savings, and concern about advertiser influence on generative-AI answers. Ipsos →

How is AI being used in election campaigns?

A report documents AI-manipulated images, cloned voices, and deepfake audio used to deceive voters and spread propaganda in campaigns in Spain, Slovakia, and the United States. Demócrata →

Why is DP World expanding its US data-center logistics network?

DP World is expanding its specialized U.S. data-center logistics network to nearly 50 sites as AI infrastructure demand grows. The expansion addresses supply-chain, security, and inventory challenges for hyperscalers. Supply Chain Digital →

Is the Pentagon creating an Autonomous Warfare Command?

A Fox News live blog reports that the Pentagon plans to establish an Autonomous Warfare Command by 2027. The item appears alongside broader regulatory scrutiny of AI superintelligence. Fox News →
