AI News for November 14, 2026

ChatGPT Wallet adds tokenized agent payments as U.S. prosecutors bring charges over a $129 million AI-impersonation scheme. Australia plans an AI safety regulator, and four nonprofits are asking a California court to freeze development of more powerful AI systems. All 86 stories from the day, below.

November 14, 202686 stories · 11 categories

U.S. prosecutors charged three foreign nationals over a $129 million corporate-fraud scheme built on AI-generated executive and lawyer impersonations. Separately, one report relayed the FBI’s claim that a North Korean group used deepfakes and fake video calls to steal more than $400 million in crypto. OpenAI, meanwhile, launched ChatGPT Wallet with card-network tokens and biometric approval, while DeepSeek opened its app’s search, URL-fetch, OCR, and speech tools through its API. The tension is plain: agents are gaining authority to spend while synthetic identity is being used to redirect money.

Australia’s Albanese government plans mandatory AI Standards enforced by a new regulator, with unions tying the debate to 850 Qantas job cuts and Ai Group warning against regulating first. In California, four nonprofits sued five AI companies seeking a freeze on more powerful systems. In Delhi, the High Court ordered the removal of defamatory AI-generated deepfakes depicting Chief Minister Rekha Gupta. And one report counted more than 206,000 tech job cuts alongside an estimated $700 billion in AI infrastructure commitments. The fight over AI’s limits is landing in regulation, court orders, and employment numbers at once.

One market brief said MediaTek raised its 2026 custom-ASIC revenue outlook from $2 billion to $2.5 billion, while Bernstein estimated Google could save about $50 billion. ETF experts told CNBC that Big Tech hyperscalers could benefit from Nvidia’s turmoil and congressional pushback on data centers. Farther down the stack, Perplexity released open-weight Decider v1.3 and Flash models, and StitchStudio raised $8 million to expand insurance-workflow agents. The money is spreading from chip supply to narrow workflow tools, though the largest savings figures remain estimates.
