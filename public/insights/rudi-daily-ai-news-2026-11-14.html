AI News for November 14, 2026

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet for agent payments over card networks. November 14, 202661 stories · 11 categories

One report says U.S. prosecutors charged three foreign nationals over a $129 million scheme that used AI-generated voices and images to impersonate executives and lawyers, with employee wire transfers later converted to cryptocurrency. Another says the FBI tied more than $400 million in stolen crypto to a North Korean group using deepfaked video calls and malware, and that agents seized the cloud servers and more than 900 victim webcam clips. Both are single-source figures worth reading skeptically. The two reports still describe concrete versions of the same risk: a false identity on the call and money moving at the other end. In India, the Delhi High Court ordered the Aam Aadmi Party to remove deepfakes of Chief Minister Rekha Gupta after ruling that they crossed from satire into defamation.

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet for tokenized agent payments over card networks, while DeepSeek opened its consumer chat tools through an API. OpenAI’s November release notes also cover the retirement of Advanced and Standard Voice in favor of GPT-Live, a new Business Hub for merchants, and Images 2.6. Perplexity released open-weight Decider v1.3 and Flash v1.3 models with a locate question type for identifying spans and Apache 2.0 licensing. As of November 13, Grok 4.8 was still in reinforcement learning, with pretraining complete, a Bot-only rollout planned and no public API date or model ID. A separate tools roundup featured llama.cpp 1.0.0, OpenCode 2.3.0 background subagents, Codex 0.169.0 and two Claude outages. Access is arriving on different clocks: a payment product is live, open-weight models are out, and Grok’s API is still undated.

Four nonprofits sued Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX’s AI unit under California’s Unfair Competition Law, seeking a judicial freeze on frontier AI development; the plaintiffs acknowledge that a real freeze would require government inspections and monitoring. ETF experts told CNBC that Big Tech hyperscalers could benefit from Nvidia’s turmoil and data-center pushback by a Democratic-controlled Congress, as investors weigh alternative AI chip suppliers and tighter compute supply. On the investment side, Jawlah reports ten startups raised about $1.88 billion, led by Mirendil’s $1 billion and LangChain’s $300 million. The UK is also amending export controls effective December 1, tightening dual-use and military lists and replacing GEA 001 with a consolidated OGEL.

What People Are Asking

What is ChatGPT Wallet?

ChatGPT Wallet is an OpenAI product for tokenized agent payments over card networks. The same report says DeepSeek opened its consumer chat tools through an API. AI Compass →

How did the $129 million AI corporate fraud scheme work?

According to one report, AI-generated voices and images impersonated executives and lawyers to trick employees into wiring funds, which were then converted to cryptocurrency. U.S. prosecutors charged three foreign nationals over the $129 million scheme. The Middle East Observer →

Did a North Korean deepfake scam steal $400 million in crypto?

According to one report, yes: the FBI says FaceStealer used deepfaked video calls and malware to steal more than $400 million from Web3 workers. The report says agents seized the cloud servers and more than 900 victim webcam clips; the dollar figure remains a single-source claim. Technwz →

Are AI labs being sued to freeze frontier AI development?

Yes. Four nonprofits sued Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX’s AI unit under California’s Unfair Competition Law, seeking a judicial freeze. The plaintiffs acknowledge that a real freeze would require government inspections and monitoring. Tech Times →

Is the Grok 4.8 API available yet?

No—not as of November 13, according to a report based on Musk’s X replies. It says pretraining was complete, reinforcement learning was continuing, a Bot-only soft rollout was planned for the week of November 10, and the public API had no date or model ID. APIYI →

What is Perplexity Decider v1.3?

Perplexity released pplx-decider-v1.3 and Flash v1.3 as open-weight decision models with a locate question type for identifying text spans, unchanged low pricing and Apache 2.0 licensing. DataStudios →

Why did the Delhi High Court order AI deepfakes removed?

The court ruled that AI-generated videos and posts depicting Chief Minister Rekha Gupta crossed from political satire into defamation and attacked her individual dignity. It ordered the Aam Aadmi Party to remove them. SCC Online →

Could Nvidia’s turmoil benefit Big Tech?

ETF experts told CNBC it could: hyperscalers may gain as investors consider alternative AI chip suppliers and tighter compute supply. They also said a Democratic-controlled Congress could push back on data centers. CNBC →
