AI News for November 7, 2026

OpenAI’s reported $11 billion round meets an $8.5 trillion data-center forecast, while courts confront AI-hallucinated judgments, prosecutors allege a $129 million impersonation fraud, and workers reconsider AI-exposed careers. All 57 stories from the day, below.

November 7, 202657 stories · 11 categories

Analytics Insight reports that OpenAI secured an initial $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation and pledged to keep expanding data centers while paying full power costs and engaging more with communities. The scale around that promise is formidable: Goldman Sachs raised its 2030 global capacity forecast to 273 GW, implying roughly $8.5 trillion in cumulative spending. A market roundup also reports ING auctioning about $500 million in data-center loans as major investors demand clearer returns, while AI-related stocks fell as investors weighed possible construction pauses. An AI Weekly roundup points to chip shortages and growing European protests. The money keeps getting larger; so do the physical and political constraints.

The consequences were less abstract elsewhere. The Bombay High Court ordered a fresh hearing after a customs order cited two apparently AI-hallucinated judgments. In the United States, prosecutors charged three people in an alleged $129 million AI impersonation scheme. Nanjing University withdrew AI classroom monitoring after a privacy backlash, and a Philadelphia Inquirer report describes young workers moving toward teaching, nursing, and skilled trades as AI-exposed career paths look less secure. Meanwhile, CBC examines whether Canada should join the race for autonomous weapons. Courts, classrooms, workplaces, and militaries are now testing where automation belongs—and who bears the risk when it fails.

What People Are Asking

How much funding did OpenAI raise?

Analytics Insight reports that OpenAI secured an initial $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation and pledged to continue expanding its data-center footprint. analyticsinsight.net →

How much data-center capacity will the world need by 2030?

Goldman Sachs raised its 2030 forecast from 217 GW to 273 GW, a projection that implies roughly $8.5 trillion in cumulative capital spending, according to MLQ News. mlq.ai →

Did an Indian court overturn an order because of AI-hallucinated cases?

A petitioner showed that a customs order relied on two untraceable, apparently AI-hallucinated Supreme Court judgments. The Bombay High Court quashed the order by consent and directed a fresh hearing. lawchakra.in →

How did the alleged $129 million AI impersonation fraud work?

Federal prosecutors charged three people with allegedly using AI-driven impersonation to defraud corporate executives of roughly $129 million. hoodline.com →

Is Anthropic watermarking Claude outputs?

According to Mezha, Anthropic is applying machine-readable watermarks to older Claude models across its products and API, with no customer opt-out, citing EU AI Act requirements and a December deadline. mezha.net →

Did a Chinese university stop using AI to monitor students?

Yes. Nanjing University withdrew a feature that tracked attendance and attentiveness after leaked screenshots triggered public criticism over surveillance and student privacy. scmp.com →

Are workers changing careers because of AI?

A Philadelphia Inquirer report describes young workers and recent graduates moving from AI-exposed white-collar paths toward teaching, nursing, and skilled trades. It cites research showing a 19% employment decline among young workers in the most AI-exposed roles. us.headtopics.com →

What are Perplexity Teammates agents?

Perplexity says Teammates can plan, coordinate, and monitor long-running work using persistent memory and progress updates. The agents are initially available on the web to Max subscribers. webpronews.com →

Is Canada developing AI-enabled autonomous weapons?

CBC examines the question rather than reporting a settled decision, weighing the military case for autonomous systems against legal and ethical concerns. cbc.ca →
