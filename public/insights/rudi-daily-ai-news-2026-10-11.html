AI News for October 11, 2026

Microsoft launches Decision-1, Nvidia’s August Nemotron 3.5 release points toward a much larger successor, one report says AM Intelligence ordered 20,000 Rubin GPUs, and an international survey finds AI adoption rising in financial planning. All 34 stories from the day, below.

October 11, 202634 stories · 9 categories

Microsoft launched Decision-1, a 9B-parameter model that scores fixed choices instead of generating prose. A weekly brief reports a Mistral Large 4 public preview and Anthropic’s Haiku 5.5 release, while Nvidia’s 30B-parameter Nemotron 3.5 Lightning shipped in August as reports point to a roughly trillion-parameter successor. Beyond the models, a daily tooling roundup covers dynamic Claude workflows, OpenCode 1.19.0 and other agent releases. The model race is producing both specialized systems and a steadily busier layer of tools around them.

According to one report, AM Intelligence placed firm orders for 20,000 Nvidia Rubin GPUs, bringing its reported commitment to roughly 29,000 chips across India and Malaysia. Klarent raised a €7.13 million seed round for agentic software testing, and an FPSB India survey found roughly eight in ten financial-planning firms use AI or plan to adopt it within a year. A cybersecurity roundup also highlighted an ASOS customer-facing SaaS breach, AI-fueled security M&A and the FBI’s move against ShinyHunters. Capital, workplace adoption and security risk all showed up in the same day’s reporting.

What People Are Asking

What is Microsoft Decision-1?

Microsoft Decision-1 is a 9B-parameter decision-scoring model post-trained from Alibaba’s Qwen3.5-9B. It returns calibrated probabilities over fixed answer choices rather than generating text. shattered.io →

Did Nvidia release Nemotron 3.5?

Yes. Nvidia shipped the open-weight, 30B-parameter Nemotron 3.5 Lightning in August 2026; reports say a roughly trillion-parameter Nemotron 4 is in development. shattered.io →

How many Nvidia Rubin GPUs did AM Intelligence order?

One report says AM Intelligence placed two firm orders for 20,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 GPUs. The same report puts its total committed Rubin fleet at roughly 29,000 chips and about 100 MW across India and Malaysia. tech-insider.org →

How much did Klarent raise for AI software testing?

Klarent raised €7.13 million in a seed round led by Mosaic Ventures. The Zurich company plans to scale its agentic software-testing platform and expand into the United States. sesamers.com →

Are eight in ten financial-planning firms already using AI?

Not necessarily: the survey groups current users with firms planning adoption. An FPSB India survey of more than 8,000 financial planners across 37 countries found roughly eight in ten firms use AI or plan to adopt it within a year, up from six in ten. theprintlines.com →

Was ASOS affected by a SaaS breach?

A daily cybersecurity roundup reports an ASOS customer-facing SaaS breach. It also highlights AI-fueled cybersecurity mergers and acquisitions and an FBI move against the ShinyHunters group. xloggs.com →

When will the 2026 AI high-end manufacturing application map be released?

It is scheduled for release on October 12 at the 2026 China International Industry Fair, during a summit forum on industrial embodied intelligence. sohu.com →

Which AI releases were highlighted in this week’s brief?

A weekly brief covers Mistral Large 4’s public preview and Anthropic’s Haiku 5.5 release. It also covers OpenAI’s Jalapeño chip-design interview, native Markdown support in Google Drive and EU AI Act deployer responsibilities. paragraph.com →
