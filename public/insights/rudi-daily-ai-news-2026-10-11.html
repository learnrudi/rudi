AI News for October 11, 2026

Nvidia released the 30B open-weight Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, GitHub put computer use into Copilot CLI, one report described a federal liability push for rogue models, and AI Funding Tracker counted about $2.39 billion in large AI rounds for the week. All 50 stories from the day, below.

October 11, 202650 stories · 9 categories

Nvidia released the 30B open-weight Nemotron 3.5 Lightning for AI agents, while reports cited in the same story say a roughly one-trillion-parameter successor is in development. GitHub added computer use to Copilot CLI and its standalone app in public preview, and Microsoft launched Decision-1, a 9B model that scores fixed answer choices instead of generating text. The models are changing shape as well as scale. Meanwhile, AM Intelligence placed firm orders for 20,000 Nvidia Rubin GPUs, committing capacity for data-center buildouts in India and Malaysia.

One report says the Trump administration's Super Intelligence Force is using liability and mandatory incident reporting to hold developers accountable for rogue models. That single-source account is worth treating cautiously, and it leaves the central question—whether creators or deployers bear responsibility—unresolved. A Congressional Research Service report likewise leaves open how Pentagon AI adoption could change force size, roles, and workforce composition. The money is moving faster than those rules: AI Funding Tracker counted about $2.39 billion across 14 rounds of at least $20 million for the week, while a survey of more than 8,000 financial planners found roughly eight in ten firms use AI or plan to adopt it within a year. Deployment is accelerating while responsibility—and the workforce it will reshape—remains unsettled.

What People Are Asking

What is Nvidia Nemotron 3.5?

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning as a 30B open-weight model for AI agents. Reports cited by the story say a roughly one-trillion-parameter Nemotron 4 successor is still in development. shattered.io →

Can GitHub Copilot CLI use a computer?

Yes, in public preview. GitHub added computer-use capability to Copilot CLI and its standalone app as competition among coding agents intensifies. tech-insider.org →

What does Microsoft Decision-1 do?

Decision-1 is Microsoft's 9B-parameter model, post-trained from Alibaba's Qwen3.5-9B, that returns calibrated probabilities over fixed answer options rather than generating text. shattered.io →

Who is liable when an AI model goes rogue?

It remains unresolved. One report says the Trump administration is using legal liability and mandatory incident reporting to hold developers accountable, but whether responsibility belongs to model creators or deployers is still an open question. news-one-nine.vercel.app →

How could AI change the US military workforce?

A Congressional Research Service report says Pentagon AI adoption could change military force size, roles, and workforce composition, but key effects remain unresolved. legis1.com →

How much did AI startups raise this week?

AI Funding Tracker counted about $2.39 billion across 14 rounds of at least $20 million, led by TypeSafe AI's $870 million Series A and Manus's raise of more than $500 million. AI Funding Tracker →

How many financial-planning firms are adopting AI?

An FPSB India survey of more than 8,000 financial planners across 37 countries found roughly eight in ten firms now use AI or plan to adopt it within a year, up from six in ten. theprintlines.com →

How many Nvidia Rubin GPUs did AM Intelligence order?

AM Intelligence placed two firm orders for 20,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 GPUs. The orders bring its total committed Rubin fleet to roughly 29,000 chips and about 100 MW across planned data-center capacity in India and Malaysia. tech-insider.org →
