Apple drags OpenAI to court over hardware secrets, a report says Anthropic cut OpenAI's access to Claude, deepfake fraud statistics spike, and an AI character heads for a feature-film debut. All 41 stories from the day, below.

July 13, 202641 stories · 9 categories

The AI labs spent the weekend turning on each other. Apple dragged OpenAI to court, accusing it of orchestrating the theft of confidential hardware secrets through two former Apple employees — the suit surfaced in a Forbes report late Sunday and is now multiply covered. And one report describes Anthropic revoking OpenAI’s API access to Claude after discovering it was being used for internal development. The model race has always been a talent war; it is now a litigation and access war too.

Synthetic media had a rough day in the trust column. One statistics roundup reports a 3,892% surge in document deepfakes alongside $893 million in US losses from AI-related complaints. Another test found Meta’s own AI-image detector misses 55% of its images once they’re cropped. Meta, meanwhile, pulled its AI feature that used public Instagram photos after three days of backlash. In the UK, the CPS admitted submitting AI-generated fake legal authorities to the High Court. And a security research report documents an AI agent running a ransomware attack end-to-end against an AI app builder — the attack surface and the attacker now made of the same stuff.

The rules kept forming around all of it. The EU AI Act’s August 2 enforcement date is three weeks out, with general-purpose AI fines and hiring tools in scope. India’s Supreme Court is drafting rules that welcome AI assistance in courts while banning it from judicial decisions. A Just Security analysis walked through US Senate proposals to regulate military AI. Hollywood argued over whether an AI character with a feature-film debut counts as an actor. Microsoft set July 24 for GPT-5.6 to become standard in Microsoft 365 Copilot. And the developer community’s quiet vote: a free configuration tool that turns Claude Code into a full dev team passed 228,000 GitHub stars — configuration expertise outdrawing raw model access.
