AI News for July 13, 2026

Apple drags OpenAI to court over hardware secrets, a report says Anthropic cut OpenAI's access to Claude, and by the close, more than 200 economists and executives — Nobel laureates among them — warn of large-scale AI job displacement. All 95 stories from the day, below.

July 13, 202695 stories · 96 source links · 10 categories

The AI labs spent the weekend turning on each other. Apple dragged OpenAI to court, accusing it of orchestrating the theft of confidential hardware secrets through two former Apple employees — the suit surfaced in a Forbes report late Sunday and is now multiply covered. And one report describes Anthropic revoking OpenAI’s API access to Claude after discovering it was being used for internal development. By the close, Bloomberg-sourced reporting said the suit is already hampering OpenAI’s recruiting and its iPhone-rival hardware program, and Anthropic extended free Claude Fable 5 access through July 19 — the rivalry’s counter-move. The model race has always been a talent war; it is now a litigation and access war too.

Synthetic media had a rough day in the trust column. One statistics roundup reports a 3,892% surge in document deepfakes alongside $893 million in US losses from AI-related complaints. Another test found Meta’s own AI-image detector misses 55% of its images once they’re cropped. Meta, meanwhile, pulled its AI feature that used public Instagram photos after three days of backlash. In the UK, the CPS admitted submitting AI-generated fake legal authorities to the High Court. And a security research report documents an AI agent running a ransomware attack end-to-end against an AI app builder — the attack surface and the attacker now made of the same stuff. Google, for its part, filed its first lawsuit against cybercriminals abusing Gemini to scale phishing operations.

The close brought the day’s weightiest document: an open letter signed by more than 200 economists and executives — Eric Schmidt and Reid Hoffman among them, and 16 Nobel laureates by one count — warning that AI could bring large-scale job displacement and urging policymakers to act now. The rules kept forming around all of it. The EU AI Act’s August 2 enforcement date is three weeks out, with general-purpose AI fines and hiring tools in scope. India’s Supreme Court is drafting rules that welcome AI assistance in courts while banning it from judicial decisions. A Just Security analysis walked through US Senate proposals to regulate military AI. Hollywood argued over whether an AI character with a feature-film debut counts as an actor. Microsoft set July 24 for GPT-5.6 to become standard in Microsoft 365 Copilot. And the developer community’s quiet vote: a free configuration tool that turns Claude Code into a full dev team passed 228,000 GitHub stars — configuration expertise outdrawing raw model access.
