About RUDI Daily.

A daily record of AI news across industries, research, and the workplace. Built by a digital worker at RUDI. Free for everyone to read.

Read RUDI Daily → Meet the digital worker

A digital worker, in practice

A digital worker that researches and assembles each edition.

RUDI Daily is an example of an AI agent doing recurring work autonomously. Its job is to search the internet for the day's significant AI stories, check the sources, and organize what it finds into a readable record.

The worker handles discovery and compilation within a defined workflow, with editorial review. Each edition includes the full set of stories that pass its checks, grouped by topic, summarized, and linked to the original reporting.

That is what we mean by a digital worker: an agent with a specific responsibility, a repeatable process, and an output people can use.

How an edition comes together

From discovery to a daily record.

  1. 01

    Search across domains

    Find the day's developments across AI models and products, industry applications, policy, infrastructure, security, work, and research.

  2. 02

    Check the date and source

    Read the source material and verify that its publisher-stated date falls on the target day. Pages with unreadable content or an unverified date are left out.

  3. 03

    Organize and summarize

    Group the stories by topic, describe what each source reports, and keep a direct link so readers can follow the evidence.

  4. 04

    Review and publish

    Bring the reporting together with editorial context in a dated edition. Keep it in the public archive so the record can be revisited over time.

What it covers

Coverage across industries, research, and public policy.

RUDI Daily follows developments across education, healthcare, business and finance, science, government, and the creative industries. It also covers the models, infrastructure, policy, and security questions that connect them.

Research papers and studies sit alongside product announcements and reporting on how people and organizations are using AI. The aim is a broad daily record with enough context to understand what changed and where to read more.

Each edition preserves the full set of stories the worker found and verified. Coverage depends on the sources available that day. Summaries describe the source's claims, with links for readers to assess them.

Earlier editions were published as the RUDI Rundown. They remain part of the same archive.

From one worker to a digital workforce

What would a digital worker do for your team?

RUDI Daily shows the pattern: define a recurring job, connect the right sources and tools, set the rules, and make the result useful. Your organization's worker could have a different job.

A digital workforce connects several workers across related responsibilities, with clear handoffs and human oversight.

Research & intelligence

Track an industry, follow regulatory updates, or assemble a source-linked briefing for your team.

Invoicing & finance

Organize incoming invoices, prepare reconciliations, and flag exceptions for someone to review.

Operations & reporting

Monitor approved inboxes, prepare recurring reports, and keep records organized across a workflow.

Working with Microsoft Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, or Codex? We can help you define the job, choose an approach, and connect the tools that fit your organization, with appropriate permissions and review.
