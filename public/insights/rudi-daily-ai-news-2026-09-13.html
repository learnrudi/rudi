AI News for September 13, 2026

Reporting on September 13 covered a split in U.S. AI regulation, Grok 4.7’s delayed release, and Bodhan AI and NVIDIA’s four open-weight Indic-language models. All 34 stories from the day, below.

September 13, 202634 stories · 9 categories

CNN reports that Democratic lawmakers, Barack Obama, and Anthropic’s CEO pushed for AI guardrails, while Trump and Speaker Johnson resisted emergency regulation. Elsewhere in education, BRICS members backed AI-enabled learning, a shared research repository, and a new university-ranking system, while Bodhan AI and NVIDIA announced four open-weight Indic-language models for India’s Bharat EduAI Stack.

The model race supplied its own friction: Grok 4.7 was delayed beyond its expected September 12 launch after Elon Musk said it needed more work on task abandonment and self-checking. Investors were more enthusiastic about AMD’s data-center revenue and AI-chip ambitions, with two analyst firms issuing Buy ratings as the shares climbed to about $516. A startup digest reported multibillion-dollar funding rounds for Poolside, Mistral, and Cognition. And an interface-design paper made a decidedly tactile proposal for the automation age: bring back actuated buttons, switches, and knobs so people can read and contest what machines are doing.

What People Are Asking

What is the U.S. political split over AI safety?

CNN reports that Democratic lawmakers, Barack Obama, and Anthropic’s CEO are calling for AI guardrails over catastrophic-risk concerns, while President Trump and Speaker Johnson resist emergency regulation and emphasize competition with China. CNN via ABC17NEWS →

When is Grok 4.7 coming out?

It was delayed beyond its expected September 12 launch. A report says Elon Musk attributed the delay to premature task abandonment and insufficient self-checking and said the model needed a few more days. apimaster.ai →

What are the four Bharat EduAI models?

Bodhan AI and NVIDIA announced Indic Transcribe, Indic OCR, Indic Speak, and Indic Translate. The four open-weight models are intended to support India’s Bharat EduAI Stack. shattered.io →

What did BRICS announce about AI in education?

BRICS members adopted the New Delhi Declaration supporting AI-enabled learning, a new BRICS university-ranking system, a shared research repository, and youth startup initiatives. indiatoday.in →

Why did AMD stock rise?

AMD shares climbed to about $516 after Piper Sandler and Stifel issued Buy ratings with price targets of $600 and $635. The firms cited record data-center CPU revenue and ambitions for AI-chip growth. ad-hoc-news.de →

How much funding did Poolside, Mistral, and Cognition raise?

A weekly startup digest reports rounds of $6 billion for Poolside, $3.5 billion for Mistral, and $2 billion for Cognition. Those figures come from a single roundup and should be read as reported amounts. neweconomies.co →

What is ALTAS for clinical LLM answers?

ALTAS is a research system that uses terminal entropy and late-layer linearity from one model pass to choose between greedy decoding and trajectory correction for each question. Its authors report TruthfulQA gains while keeping clinical QA benchmarks within a do-no-harm band. arxiv.org →
