AI News for October 19, 2026

An IMF report finds AI-exposed labor demand across Asia down nearly 10% since November 2022, Illinois creates an AI Cabinet, Perplexity open-sources a secure agent sandbox, and NVIDIA packages Nemotron 3.5 Bolt for local agentic work. All 39 stories from the day, below.

October 19, 202639 stories · 40 source links · 8 categories

An IMF report finds that labor demand in AI-exposed occupations across Asia has fallen nearly 10% since November 2022, even as adoption creates specialized roles and advances unevenly across companies. A Fortune report says executives are reconsidering how they measure AI’s success and whether workers share its productivity gains. The technology may be changing the work; employers still have to decide who benefits.

Illinois Governor JB Pritzker created an AI Cabinet to assess risks and develop regulatory proposals through 2027. At the federal level, a Defense Department official said uncooperative AI labs would face export controls. Meanwhile, AI Weekly reports that House Democrats are planning an AI oversight push. Government’s leverage over AI is taking shape through state proposals, congressional oversight, and access to global markets.

Perplexity open-sourced SPACE, a sandbox for running autonomous agents with isolated execution and access controls. The AICPA and AIUC announced a certification combining CPA-led attestation with live agent-security testing, with full launch expected in early 2027. And NVIDIA’s Nemotron 3.5 Bolt compresses an open-weight model into 24GB for local agentic tasks. Agents are moving closer to local machines and real workflows, while the controls meant to contain them race to catch up.

What People Are Asking

Is AI reducing employment demand in Asia?

An IMF October 2026 report finds that labor demand in AI-exposed occupations across Asia has fallen nearly 10% since November 2022. It also says AI adoption is creating specialized roles and remains uneven across companies. insightsonindia.com →

Are employees benefiting from the AI productivity boom?

The report says executives are reassessing both how they measure AI’s success and whether employees share in the productivity gains. biztoc.com →

What will the Illinois AI Cabinet do?

It will assess AI risks and develop regulatory proposals through 2027, building on the state’s AI Safety Measures Act. insurancejournal.com →

Could AI labs face US export controls?

A U.S. Defense Department official, Emil Michael, said export controls would be imposed on AI labs that do not cooperate with the government. techflowpost.com →

What is Perplexity SPACE?

SPACE is Perplexity’s open-source secure sandbox for autonomous AI agents, providing isolated execution and access controls. thedailystar.net →

What is the AICPA and AIUC certification for AI agents?

It combines CPA-led SOC 2 attestation with live AI-agent security testing under AIUC-1. The organizations expect a full launch in early 2027. prnewswire.com →

What is NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt?

It is a compressed 24GB open-weight model derived from Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and designed for local agentic tasks on RTX and DGX Spark hardware. time.news →

Can optical interconnects cut AI training time by 38%?

Kopin’s white paper models a 1.3–1.6x increase in useful training throughput and up to 38% less training time from its optical-interconnect architecture. Those are company modeling claims, not independently established results. stocktitan.net →
