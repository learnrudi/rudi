AI News for October 18, 2026

A Wesleyan study tracks $137 million behind AI-made political ads, Claude Code gets official plugins and longer-running cloud sessions, Perplexity releases a model built to return judgments rather than answers, and security reporting spans infrastructure hacks and an alleged insider operation. All 25 stories from the day, below.

October 18, 202625 stories · 8 categories

A Wesleyan Media Project study counted 220 AI-generated or AI-enhanced political ads backed by more than $137 million ahead of the 2026 U.S. midterms. Republican sponsors dominated the spending, while only 27% of the ads disclosed AI use. In developer tools, Anthropic made Claude Code plugins official and added a JavaScript/TypeScript customization system plus cloud sessions that can keep coding tasks running beyond the terminal. Perplexity took a narrower route with a 27B model that returns judgment probabilities instead of answers; its reported benchmark gains still need independent validation.

The rest of the field was busy at several layers. Samsung reportedly tapped TSMC’s 3nm process for Nvidia HBM base dies, while Indian AI startups raised about $50.6 million in one week, led by RapidCanvas’s $29 million Series B. A security roundup covered hacks targeting European supermarket refrigeration units and an alleged OpenAI insider recording operation. Meanwhile, a wider weekly brief gathered model launches from Meta, NVIDIA, Vivix, and others, alongside infrastructure and workplace-adoption debates.
