AI News for October 23, 2026

China proposes export controls covering 23 AI-related technologies, music publishers press a lyrics-training suit against Anthropic, lawmakers describe an AI-accelerated Russian influence campaign, and colleges race to launch undergraduate AI majors. All 28 stories from the day, below.

October 23, 202628 stories · 9 categories

China proposed adding 40 technologies—including 23 related to AI—to its export-control catalogue, while updated controls will require authorization for certain UAVs and related components beginning November 18. Music publishers including Universal Music Group are suing Anthropic over its use of song lyrics to train Claude; Anthropic argued fair use at a California federal hearing that could set a precedent for AI copyright cases. A California bill regulating attorneys’ use of generative AI is also heading to Governor Newsom. The rules around what AI may absorb, export, and produce are being written through different institutions—and with different consequences.

Two Democratic lawmakers say a classified briefing revealed an AI-accelerated Russian disinformation campaign targeting pro-Ukraine candidates ahead of the U.S. midterms. At the same time, more voters are turning to chatbots for candidate and ballot information, raising concerns about inaccurate answers, misinformation, and outside influence. Financial regulators are warning about another form of synthetic persuasion: AI-powered impersonation fraud using deepfake audio, video, and text. The risk is circular: voters and investors are consulting AI while other actors use the same technology to deceive them.

Colleges in Massachusetts and across the country are launching undergraduate AI majors as students worry about jobs and employers seek AI skills. The hardware underneath that demand is growing too: one daily roundup reports a 1.07-gigawatt Google virtual-power-plant deal and Nvidia’s 64GB DGX Spark launch. Outside the data center, Hyundai says its India-spec Bayon SUV will arrive October 26 with a Perplexity-powered generative AI voice assistant. Students are choosing degrees around AI while manufacturers are placing it directly in the products those graduates may build.

What People Are Asking

What AI technologies is China proposing to place under export controls?

China’s MOFCOM and MOST proposed adding 40 technologies to the country’s export-control catalogue, including 23 related to AI. Updated controls on UAVs and related components are set to require authorization beginning November 18, 2026. Expeditors →

Is Anthropic being sued for training Claude on song lyrics?

Yes. Music publishers including Universal Music Group are suing Anthropic over training Claude on song lyrics, while Anthropic argued at a California federal hearing that the training qualifies as fair use. Seoul Economic Daily →

Is Russia using AI to target candidates in the U.S. midterms?

According to two Democratic lawmakers, a classified intelligence briefing described an AI-accelerated Russian online disinformation campaign aimed at pro-Ukraine candidates before the U.S. midterms. Kyiv Independent →

Can voters trust AI chatbots for election information?

More voters are using chatbots for candidate and ballot information, but the reporting raises concerns about inaccurate answers, misinformation, and outside influence on their decisions. NewsNation →

How much money was lost to AI-related internet crime in 2025?

The FBI reported nearly $900 million in AI-related internet-crime losses in 2025, according to coverage of a joint Investor Bulletin from the SEC and five partner regulators. The regulators warn that increasingly sophisticated AI impersonation fraud could cause billions in losses. thetechedvocate.org →

Are colleges starting to offer AI as an undergraduate major?

Yes. Institutions in Massachusetts and elsewhere are rapidly introducing undergraduate AI majors, driven by students’ job-market anxiety and employers’ demand for workers with AI skills. WBUR →

Will the Hyundai Bayon have a Perplexity AI assistant?

Hyundai says the India-spec Bayon will launch October 26 with a Perplexity-powered generative AI voice assistant, SoundHound voice technology, an NVIDIA-accelerated connected cockpit, and Bluelink services. Moneycontrol →

What happened to Thomson Reuters court software?

A Law360 Pulse roundup reports that a cyberattack on Thomson Reuters court software affected courts in 11 U.S. states and Canada. Law360 Pulse →
