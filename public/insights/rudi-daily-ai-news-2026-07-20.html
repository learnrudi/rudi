July 20, 202640 stories · 8 categories

AMD put hardware behind years of talk about challenging Nvidia. Its first rack-scale AI system, Helios, is headed for delivery with Microsoft as its newest customer, joining Meta, OpenAI, and Oracle on the buyer list. AMD shares rose more than 3%, and one report calls Helios the first strong rival Nvidia has faced in years — a judgment deliveries, not announcements, will settle.

The governance ledger ran from statehouse to street. Illinois enacted an AI Safety Measures Act imposing governance, transparency, audit, and incident-reporting obligations on developers of advanced frontier models — a state law reaching directly into the frontier tier. At the UN, leaders argue global AI governance cannot wait as the digital divide widens, pressing for guardrails and capacity building in developing countries. And Vox examines the rise of AI populism — protests, and in some cases violence, aimed at AI and data centers, driven by anger over corporate power exercised without democratic input.

The agent economy started metering. Microsoft’s Copilot Cowork is now a metered agent that consumes credits as it rolls out globally, with admin controls for deployment and spending. xAI shipped a free Grok for Excel add-in powered by Grok 4.5. SAP’s quarter stacked Joule Work, an Autonomous Suite, and Industry AI. The demand side explains the urgency: McKinsey finds half of consumers now start product research in AI search, which moves brands from chasing rankings to chasing citations. And in the lab, a survey of biopharma executives credits AI with cutting early drug-development costs and timelines by up to 70%.
