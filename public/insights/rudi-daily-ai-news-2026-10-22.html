AI News for October 22, 2026

Barclays ties thousands of job cuts to AI automation, Anthropic launches Claude Duet, UNIDIR warns about shrinking human control in military decisions, and Italy’s workplace AI mandate takes effect October 23. All 33 stories from the day, below.

October 22, 202633 stories · 9 categories

Barclays announced thousands of job cuts across banking, customer support, and administrative roles, explicitly tying the reductions to AI and automated software agents. Anthropic, meanwhile, launched Claude Duet as a proactive background agent for task management and workflow automation, while Thomson Reuters rebuilt CoCounsel around agents that can plan and complete tax and audit work for professional review. The products promise more work done by software; Barclays supplied the bluntest account of what that can mean for workers.

The head of UNIDIR warned that AI is crowding human judgment out of military decisions involving targeting, drones, and nuclear systems, as the UN presses for a binding treaty on lethal autonomous weapons. In the workplace, Italy’s new rule requires employers to include AI systems that affect work organization in mandatory safety assessments beginning October 23. A Fox News live blog also covered Senate permitting reform for the power demands of AI data centers. AI policy is moving from broad principles into the places where systems receive power, organize labor, and make consequential decisions.

What People Are Asking

What is Anthropic’s Claude Duet?

Claude Duet is a proactive background AI agent built on Claude Opus 5.5 for task management and workflow automation. The launch positions Anthropic against OpenAI Dots and Meta Muse. itechpost.com →

Why is Barclays cutting thousands of jobs?

Barclays said the reductions span banking, customer support, and administrative roles, and explicitly attributed them to faster adoption of AI and automated software agents. Labor unions criticized the cuts. oodaloop.com →

What changed in Thomson Reuters CoCounsel?

The rebuilt CoCounsel uses AI agents, Checkpoint research, and code execution to plan and complete tax and audit tasks for professional review. siliconangle.com →

What does Italy’s AI workplace safety mandate require?

Beginning October 23, employers must assess AI systems that affect work organization within mandatory workplace safety documentation. The rule has no transition period and will be enforced through domestic labor inspection. techtimes.com →

What is the UN warning about military AI?

The head of UNIDIR warned that AI is increasingly displacing human judgment in military operations involving targeting, drones, and nuclear systems. The UN is seeking a legally binding treaty on lethal autonomous weapons. sofx.com →

Which Gemini model did GitLab add to Duo?

A Fox News live blog covers Senate permitting reform aimed at the power needs of AI data centers, alongside other AI safety, defense, retail, and infrastructure developments. foxnews.com →
