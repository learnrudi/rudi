AI News for November 24, 2026

One weekly roundup says GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol shipped to mixed reviews as Google launched Gemini 4 Neon and Anthropic became public. Other reports flag 35,000 exposed Grok Build applications, more than 10,000 AI-built fake shops, and a U.S. court ruling that let a wrongful-arrest suit proceed after police relied solely on facial recognition. All 54 stories from the day, below.

November 24, 202654 stories · 58 source links · 10 categories

One weekly roundup says OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol to mixed reviews while Google launched Gemini 4 Neon, Anthropic became public, and Microsoft introduced in-house models. Notebookcheck reports that GPT-6.2 is also reaching ChatGPT’s regular chat for paid subscribers, while Free and Go users remain on GPT-6 Luna. The releases arrived beside proposals for restraint: AI Impact Hub says OpenAI proposed shared development limits and prediction-loss measurements, while ChainCatcher reports that some Anthropic and OpenAI employees believe unpublished models are too powerful to release safely. The model race now includes an argument among its own builders about when capability should stop outrunning control.

The risk story was already outside the labs. One roundup reports a Grok Build security exposure affecting 35,000 applications, a single-source figure worth treating cautiously. A separate report describes more than 10,000 AI-built counterfeit shops imitating 166 brands across 26 countries, with some appearing in AI shopping recommendations. A U.S. federal court let a wrongful-arrest lawsuit proceed after police relied solely on facial recognition, while a Congressional Research Service report questioned the Defense Department’s readiness for agentic cyberattacks. In Nigeria, the UN and national human-rights officials warned that AI-generated misinformation could threaten the 2027 elections. Breaches, scams, unlawful arrests, defense gaps, and election interference put different institutions on the same assignment: verify before acting.

Workplaces and schools are preparing for systems that make more decisions. An MIT CTL and Mecalux study of 2,000 supply-chain professionals found that 57% expect AI to autonomously handle more than a quarter of warehouse decisions by 2028, while 73% are upskilling floor employees. Tamil Nadu launched a 40-hour AI and ICT program for 2,000 teachers, and Iran’s education minister said roughly 250,000 teachers received AI training last year. The infrastructure underneath that expansion remains constrained: AI Weekly reports tight TSMC advanced-node capacity through 2027, while L&T’s LTN Compute secured another multi-year Nvidia B300 order for its Chennai data center. Training people and reserving compute are becoming parallel parts of the same rollout.

What People Are Asking

Is GPT-6.2 available in ChatGPT?

Yes—for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscribers. Notebookcheck reports that GPT-6.2 is rolling out to regular ChatGPT chat with improved instruction-following, image understanding, and teen safety; Free and Go users remain on GPT-6 Luna. notebookcheck.net →

What did the court rule about arrests based on AI facial recognition?

A U.S. federal court rejected a Kentucky detective’s immunity claim after an innocent man was arrested solely on an AI facial-recognition result. The court said that reliance could be unconstitutional and allowed the wrongful-arrest lawsuit to proceed. digitaltoday.co.kr →

Were 35,000 Grok Build applications exposed?

According to one roundup, yes: a Grok Build security exposure affected 35,000 applications. That is a single-source figure and should be read cautiously. wiretensors.ai →

How many AI-built fake shops were found before Black Friday?

One report describes a network of more than 10,000 AI-built counterfeit shops imitating 166 brands across 26 countries. Some of the sites reportedly appeared in AI shopping-chatbot recommendations. removeyourmedia.com →

Are AI lab employees calling for a pause in model training?

Some are, according to one report. ChainCatcher says employees at Anthropic and OpenAI described unpublished models as too powerful to release safely, with some suggesting a pause in new model training; neither company is reported to have announced such a pause. chaincatcher.com →

How much warehouse decision-making could AI handle by 2028?

In an MIT CTL and Mecalux study, 57% of 2,000 supply-chain professionals expected AI to autonomously handle more than a quarter of warehouse decisions by 2028. The study also found that 73% were upskilling warehouse-floor employees. esmmagazine.com →

Is AI misinformation a concern for Nigeria’s 2027 elections?

Yes. The UN and Nigeria’s National Human Rights Commission warned that AI-generated fake content could threaten democratic stability before the 2027 elections, and proposed an information-integrity situation room and a voluntary media code. leadership.ng →

What is Congress being asked about agentic AI cyberattacks?

A Congressional Research Service report asks whether the Defense Department is properly equipped to detect and respond to agentic AI cyberattacks. It cites a disputed Chinese state-sponsored campaign and DARPA’s AI Cyber Challenge. legis1.com →
