AI News for October 24, 2026

One report says Amazon is warehousing unpowered “dark GPUs” as Google Cloud targets A5X deployments at enormous scale; banks plan for autonomous-agent fallout, U.S. transit agencies revisit driverless trains, and Spain confronts an election deepfake. All 30 stories from the day, below.

October 24, 202630 stories · 10 categories

According to one report, Amazon is buying billions of dollars of NVIDIA racks each month that it cannot yet power, leaving the hardware in storage. That single-source figure deserves skepticism, but the constraint it describes is concrete: electricity, not access to chips. Google Cloud and NVIDIA announced November availability and scaling targets of up to 960,000 GPUs for A5X, while a funding roundup says Tenstorrent raised $1.5 billion at a $6.2 billion valuation. At the other end of the hardware spectrum, NVIDIA released an open small language model aimed at local voice agents, game characters, and robots. Money, racks, and models are arriving; powered capacity remains a separate problem.

Major U.S. banks are drawing up contingency plans for possible disruption as autonomous agents gain access to customer accounts. In public infrastructure, Washington, D.C., and Los Angeles are pursuing driverless subway lines as unions raise safety and job concerns. In Spain, an AI-generated opposition ad depicting the prime minister’s wife and Socialist figures prompted legal complaints and condemnation. Even model news is arriving before formal disclosure: GitHub activity suggests Alibaba’s Qwen4 architecture is spreading, although no release date or model card has been announced. Finance, transit, campaigns, and model distribution are all testing how much autonomy or uncertainty institutions will tolerate.

What People Are Asking

Why is Amazon warehousing “dark GPUs”?

According to one report, Amazon lacks enough data-center power to operate all the NVIDIA racks it is purchasing, so some hardware is being warehoused. The claim involves billions of dollars in monthly purchases and should be treated as a single-source figure. TechSpot →

How many GPUs will Google Cloud A5X support?

Google Cloud and NVIDIA announced scaling targets of up to 80,000 GPUs per site and 960,000 across sites, with A5X general availability planned for November 2026. shattered.io →

What is NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B?

It is an open 8-billion-parameter model with 4 billion effective parameters, trained through reinforcement learning from Gemma 4 E4B. NVIDIA is targeting local voice agents, game characters, and robots running on RTX, Jetson, and Spark hardware. MarkTechPost →

When will Alibaba release Qwen4?

No release date has been announced. GitHub pull requests and inference-library support suggest the Qwen4-Exp architecture is spreading ahead of an official release, but there is not yet a model card. Startup Fortune →

Are banks preparing for AI-related financial instability?

Yes. Futurism reports that major U.S. banks are developing contingency plans for possible instability as autonomous agents increasingly act on customers’ accounts. Futurism →

Will U.S. subway systems run without drivers?

Possibly. Washington, D.C., and Los Angeles are pushing to develop driverless subway lines, while unions are resisting over safety and potential job losses. The Daily Reporter →

Did Spain’s PP use an AI deepfake in the election campaign?

Yes. The English Chronicle reports that Spain’s opposition PP released an AI-generated ad depicting Prime Minister Sánchez’s wife and other Socialist figures, prompting legal complaints and condemnation ahead of the 29 November vote. The English Chronicle →

How much did Tenstorrent raise?

A funding roundup reports that Tenstorrent raised a $1.5 billion Series E at a $6.2 billion valuation. It says the week’s AI funding exceeded $3.7 billion across several rounds. tech-insider.org →
