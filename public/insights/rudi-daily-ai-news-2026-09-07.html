AI News for September 7, 2026

OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra with advanced computer-use, coding, and cybersecurity capabilities, while its critical cybersecurity risk level draws scrutiny. Copyright fights, data-center investment, digital identity, and labor protections show where the new capabilities meet real-world constraints. All 46 stories from the day, below.

September 7, 202646 stories · 10 categories

OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra with advanced computer-use, coding, and cybersecurity capabilities, while also placing the model at a critical cybersecurity risk level. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang declared that AGI has arrived, a conclusion critics and researchers dispute. The launch lands amid a fight over the material used to build these systems: OpenAI and Microsoft argue that training on copyrighted news is fair use, while the Seattle Times and Newsday have sued them over using articles without permission. Capability is moving quickly; agreement about what it means and what it may lawfully consume is not.

The physical and institutional costs are just as visible. PwC projects $31.6 trillion in global data-center investment by 2050, with power supply as a key constraint. Tech companies say closed-loop cooling can sharply reduce data-center water use, though reporting gaps and trade-offs remain. AI-enabled fraud is also accelerating U.S. adoption of cryptographic digital identity credentials, while labor unions pursue worker protections even as building trades benefit from the data-center boom. The systems may be digital, but their constraints have become stubbornly physical, legal, and human.
