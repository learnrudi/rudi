AI News for October 8, 2026

The U.S. Army expands a $1.8 billion AI battle network, lawmakers press for mandatory safety rules and Pentagon oversight, AI-search citations prove easy to manipulate, and publishers bring another major copyright suit. All 63 stories from the day, below.

October 8, 202663 stories · 10 categories

The U.S. Army is expanding a $1.8 billion AI battle network into the Indo-Pacific, with nine software vendors building on Anduril’s Lattice architecture to connect forces and weapons. That deployment sits beside a sharper argument over control: Sen. Maria Cantwell proposed six mandatory frontier-AI safety principles, while a bipartisan Senate bill would require continuous Pentagon vetting of commercial frontier-model contractors. The systems are moving into consequential settings; lawmakers are trying to decide what scrutiny should follow them there.

The information layer looked fragile. A measurement study found that ordinary posts on low-barrier sites entered citations across 10 AI-search platforms, including a fabricated concept cited by eight platforms within seven days and a $14 purchase cited within an hour. Attackers are exploiting trust more directly too: researchers uncovered fake ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude portals designed to steal passwords and live MFA codes. One problem corrupts what an AI system cites; the other borrows AI brands to corrupt what a person trusts.

Publishers and platforms were also fighting over who bears the cost of AI use. USA Today Co. and several local newspapers sued OpenAI, alleging that hundreds of thousands of articles were copied for model training and seeking more than $250 million. Separately, a proposed class action alleges DraftKings used AI to target vulnerable gamblers with promotions while shelving another tool intended to flag addiction risk. Both are allegations, not verdicts, but each asks the same practical question: who is accountable when an AI-enabled business system causes harm?

What People Are Asking

What is the U.S. Army’s $1.8 billion AI battle network?

It is expanding a $1.8 billion Next Generation Command and Control network to I Corps in the Indo-Pacific, with contracts awarded to nine software vendors and an architecture built on Anduril’s Lattice. armyrecognition.com →

How would the Senate bill expand Pentagon oversight of frontier AI?

The proposal would require the Pentagon to continuously assess commercial frontier-AI contractors for security vulnerabilities and establish new reporting requirements intended to reduce national-security risks. defensescoop.com →

Is Washington moving toward mandatory AI safety rules?

Sen. Maria Cantwell unveiled six principles for mandatory frontier-AI safety standards, challenging the White House’s voluntary approach and signaling greater regulatory pressure ahead of the midterms. pymnts.com →

How easy is it to manipulate AI-search citations?

A study found that low-barrier public posts could enter citations across 10 AI-search platforms. A fabricated concept was cited by eight of the 10 platforms within seven days, while a $14 GEO purchase produced a citation within an hour. arxiv.org →

Can fake ChatGPT and Gemini ads steal MFA codes?

Researchers uncovered a human-operated phishing platform that impersonates major AI brands through browser-in-the-browser portals to capture passwords and live MFA codes. tech-insider.org →

Why is USA Today suing OpenAI?

USA Today Co. and several of its local newspapers allege that OpenAI copied hundreds of thousands of articles to train AI models. They are seeking more than $250 million in damages. theverge.com →

What does the DraftKings AI lawsuit allege?

The proposed class action alleges that DraftKings used a machine-learning model to identify vulnerable gamblers and target them with promotions encouraging greater losses, while shelving a separate tool designed to flag addiction risk. classaction.org →

Which outside AI models will Grok Bot use?

Elon Musk says the bot will route tasks to Suno for music, Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5.5, and Midjourney. Timelines remain unclear, and Suno does not have a public API. musicbusinessworldwide.com →
