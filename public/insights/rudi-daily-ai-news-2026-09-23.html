AI News for September 23, 2026

Anthropic’s Opus 5.5 and OpenAI’s GPT-6 Sol and Luna accelerate the model-release race, while autonomous cyberattacks test digital trust, military logistics adopt randomized routing, and five pharmaceutical rivals train across private data. All 61 stories from the day, below.

September 23, 202661 stories · 10 categories

Anthropic’s Opus 5.5 and OpenAI’s GPT-6 Sol and Luna arrived 90 minutes apart, according to one report, pairing faster releases with lower prices. Another report says the OpenAI models cut API prices by 50% versus GPT-5.6 promotional rates, while Alibaba says Qwen 4 remains in training without a release date. The frontier-model calendar is getting crowded before every promised model even has a date.

The harder problem was trust. A Trellix report describes autonomous espionage agents, deepfake payment fraud, mobile malware, and checkout skimming designed to make malicious interactions look normal. U.S. TRANSCOM is responding to a different threat model with randomized AI routing, autonomous network healing, sensors, and digital twins. Meanwhile, Nebius raised prices for GPU, CPU, and memory capacity, putting a bill on the compute shortage.

AI collaboration also moved across organizational boundaries. Five pharmaceutical rivals trained a federated model on more than 20,000 proprietary structures without sharing their data, while unions pursued job-security provisions, retraining, and transparency rules. Runway launched DIFFUSE to connect AI-native creatives with brands and studios, citing internal research that 17% of creative job postings require AI skills. The technology is crossing company lines; the terms of participation are still being negotiated.
