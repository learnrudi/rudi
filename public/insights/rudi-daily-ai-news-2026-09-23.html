GPT-6 Sol and Luna enter a fast-moving model price war, California pursues an emergency AI “kill switch,” AI-made political ads test voter trust, and rising compute costs sharpen the case for private infrastructure. All 92 stories from the day, below.

September 23, 202692 stories · 10 categories

OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and Luna at API prices 50% below GPT-5.6 promotional rates. Another account says Anthropic’s Opus 5.5 and OpenAI’s new models arrived 90 minutes apart, while broader reporting describes an accelerating price war between cheaper frontier models. The release pace is the point: the leading labs keep shipping despite their own calls for restraint.

The policy response pulled in opposite directions. California named experts to advance independent oversight and an emergency “kill switch” for rogue frontier models, while Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar proposed a ban on artificial superintelligence, albeit with long odds in Congress. President Trump, meanwhile, called AI “Super Intelligence” and rejected safety-driven slowdowns. Outside Washington, AI-generated political ads placed candidates in fabricated situations ahead of the U.S. midterms, with little regulation and uncertain electoral effects.

Infrastructure supplied the less theatrical constraint. Nebius raised memory prices about 41% as compute demand outpaced supply, while Go.AI raised $85 million for on-premises AI systems aimed at institutions keeping sensitive data in-house. Five pharmaceutical rivals showed what that separation can enable by training a shared drug-discovery model without exchanging raw proprietary data. The security stakes were equally concrete: a Trellix report describes autonomous espionage, mobile malware, deepfake fraud, and checkout skimming. Cheaper models may be arriving quickly; trusted deployment is still the expensive part.

Frontiers | Data-driven vestibular fMRI in PPPD and residual dizziness after BPPV: a narrative methodological review of AI-based phenotyping and longitudinal modelingfrontiersin.org

A narrative methodological review proposes a framework combining fMRI, AI, and statistical modeling for phenotyping PPPD and tracking residual dizziness after BPPV, while stressing that current findings remain hypothesis-generating.

AlphaFold 3: Mapping Complex Biomolecular Interactionsscience-technology.news-articles.net

A Nature-derived analysis explains how AlphaFold 3's diffusion architecture predicts protein-DNA, RNA, and ligand interactions while raising open-science concerns over restricted model access, with implications for drug discovery and a future digital twin of the cell.
