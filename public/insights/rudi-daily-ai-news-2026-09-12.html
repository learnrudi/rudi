AI News for September 12, 2026

Salesforce announced long-horizon agents, Anthropic’s CEO urged an AI slowdown, Bodhan AI and its partners released open-weight education models, and a funding roundup reported an $875 million raise for Positron AI. All 56 stories from the day, below.

September 12, 202656 stories · 57 source links · 11 categories

Salesforce’s September 11 announcement paired seven prebuilt AI agents with a runtime designed to pursue goals across days and weeks, moving enterprise agents beyond a single chat. Slack’s new voice-activated dashboards and reports bring a similar shift into Slackbot, where connected services such as Salesforce and Google Drive supply the working data. PBS reports that Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei urged the industry to slow development so safety measures can catch up; his warning that AI could enable a swarm of agents capable of taking over the internet within six to twelve months is a single-source scenario, not an independently confirmed forecast. A separate report says xAI delayed Grok 4.7 again over reinforcement-learning problems involving reasoning rigor and premature task abandonment; that report covers a September 11 delay.

Models and capital also moved toward narrower jobs. Bodhan AI, NVIDIA and AI4Bharat released open-weight models for Indian-language speech, translation and OCR for the proposed Bharat EduAI Stack in education. A funding roundup reported a $1.5 billion Pinegrove fund, a $70 million Inspiren raise and $875 million for Positron AI. NextDocs explained the recent model wave for document work and identified the models its product runs, while an AI implementation consultant recommended that accountancy firms automate bank reconciliation, document chasing and AML onboarding checks first while leaving tax judgment and client advisory work to people. The common thread was specificity: longer-running agents, language-focused models, inference hardware and tightly bounded office work.
