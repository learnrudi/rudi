AI News for August 25, 2026

Perplexity and NVIDIA bring an AI agent to local GPUs, one report ties Alibaba’s Wan3.0 to a $10.2 billion infrastructure share placement, TRM Labs reports deepfake scam losses up 263%, and Nvidia details its 88-core Vera CPU. All 69 stories from the day, below.

August 25, 202669 stories · 10 categories

Perplexity and NVIDIA are releasing Portable Computer for consumer GPUs with at least 32GB of VRAM, with privacy and cost savings as the pitch. A separate report describes the agent as already released with zero token costs; the accounts differ on launch status, so the safest conclusion is that local execution—not a metered cloud service—is the product’s defining claim. Apple announced M5 Ultra and M6 chips with on-device AI capabilities, Nvidia detailed an 88-core Vera CPU for AI data centers, and one report says Alibaba paired Wan3.0’s document-to-video capability with a $10.2 billion share placement for AI infrastructure. The hardware story spans a desk, a data center, and the funding required to keep both supplied.

TRM Labs reports deepfake scam losses are up 263%, a single-source figure worth reading skeptically, and says attackers increasingly manipulate users rather than hack code. A report on banks says recent disruptions have made resilience against cyberattacks and geopolitical shocks part of the AI adoption decision. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella warned businesses against outsourcing their thinking to one AI model and argued for data control, while Bain and Anthropic partnered to help enterprises deploy Claude models across operations. An agentic-AI preprint found that stronger models and more reasoning raised costs without improving predictive performance. The common work is concrete: detect manipulation, preserve resilience, retain data control, and check whether extra reasoning buys a better result.
