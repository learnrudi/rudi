From scattered activity to operating capacity

Move beyond training and tool access.

The playbook turns workplace AI enablement into a sequence of decisions, artifacts, and gates an organization can actually work through.

01

Organize ownership

Name the sponsor, enablement lead, council, decision rights, and practical guidance.

02

Assess honestly

Establish a baseline across literacy, adoption, readiness, risk, and opportunity.

03

Redesign real work

Map workflows, decompose tasks, and decide where to avoid, augment, automate, or add.

04

Evaluate responsibly

Test use cases, vendors, and designs against human-centered criteria and organizational constraints.

05

Run disciplined pilots

Prioritize opportunities, define owners and measures, and make scale-or-stop decisions explicit.

06

Build durable capacity

Support adoption, measure outcomes, share learning, and improve the operating system over time.

Formats, use, and support

Frequently asked questions.

Does the free edition include all 15 worksheets?

Yes. The public digital edition includes the complete playbook and all 15 browser-editable worksheets. The worksheets can be filled in locally in the browser and printed; nothing is saved to a server.

Why are the Playbook and Worksheet Toolkit separate?

The 43-page portrait Playbook is designed for reading and reference. It includes a worksheet index and references, but not the full worksheet pages. The separate Toolkit contains 19 landscape sheets covering all 15 worksheets so they can remain loose, be copied, or be printed individually as needed.

Can I download and print the materials?

Yes. Download the free 43-page Playbook PDF, the complete 19-sheet Worksheet Toolkit PDF, or any individual worksheet PDF. The Playbook prints portrait and double-sided; the worksheets print landscape and single-sided.

When will printed products be available?

The Printed Field Guide, Printed Worksheet Toolkit, Working Bundle, pricing, and fulfillment approach are being finalized. Choose the relevant print product to register your interest and RUDI will follow up when its release is confirmed.

What use or licensing is included?

The public edition is available to read and work through on its published site. Team-scale distribution, packaged facilitator materials, and broader reuse are handled through the Enablement Kit or Team Pack so the right terms and support can match the intended use.

What is the difference between a workshop and an Enablement Sprint?

A workshop creates shared understanding and applies selected parts of the framework to your organization. An Enablement Sprint goes further: it is a scoped implementation engagement designed to organize the work, establish a baseline, prioritize opportunities, and activate the next steps.

Do we need to complete the playbook before contacting RUDI?

No. Bring the situation as it is. RUDI can help you choose the right entry point, whether that is a self-directed resource, team package, workshop, or implementation sprint.
