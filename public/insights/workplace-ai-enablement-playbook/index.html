From scattered activity to operating capacity

Move beyond training and tool access.

The playbook turns workplace AI enablement into a sequence of decisions, artifacts, and gates an organization can actually work through.

01

Organize ownership

Name the sponsor, enablement lead, council, decision rights, and practical guidance.

02

Assess honestly

Establish a baseline across literacy, adoption, readiness, risk, and opportunity.

03

Redesign real work

Map workflows, decompose tasks, and decide where to avoid, augment, automate, or add.

04

Evaluate responsibly

Test use cases, vendors, and designs against human-centered criteria and organizational constraints.

05

Run disciplined pilots

Prioritize opportunities, define owners and measures, and make scale-or-stop decisions explicit.

06

Build durable capacity

Support adoption, measure outcomes, share learning, and improve the operating system over time.

Formats, use, and support

Frequently asked questions.

Does the free edition include all 15 worksheets?

The free web edition includes the full playbook, with a separate downloadable toolkit containing all 15 worksheets across 19 landscape sheets. Each worksheet also opens as its own standalone page above.

Why are the Playbook and Worksheet Toolkit separate?

The 43-page portrait Playbook is designed for reading and reference. It includes the worksheet index and references, but not the full worksheet pages. The separate Toolkit contains 19 landscape sheets covering all 15 worksheets so they can remain loose, be copied, or be printed individually as needed.

Can I download and print the materials?

Yes. Download the free 43-page Playbook PDF, the complete 19-sheet Worksheet Toolkit PDF, or open any individual worksheet page for focused saving and printing. The Playbook prints portrait and double-sided; the worksheets print landscape and single-sided.

What is the difference between the two printed editions?

The Printed Field Guide is a premium bound reading and reference guide. The Printed Working Edition is a lay-flat format built for the worksheets, writing space, and repeated work. Expected ranges are $49–$69 for the Field Guide and $69–$99 for the Working Edition.

What is included in the RUDI Enablement Kit and Team Pack?

The $195–$495 Enablement Kit includes the Working Edition, editable templates, facilitator deck, rollout plan, and updates. The $1,000–$3,000 Team Pack equips 10–25 books with an internal license and facilitation materials.

What is the difference between a workshop and an Enablement Sprint?

A $2,500–$7,500 workshop creates shared understanding and applies selected parts of the framework to your organization. A $7,500+ Enablement Sprint goes further: it is a scoped implementation engagement designed to organize the work, establish a baseline, prioritize opportunities, and activate the next steps.

Do we need to complete the playbook before contacting RUDI?

No. Bring the situation as it is. RUDI can help you choose the right entry point, whether that is a self-directed resource, team package, workshop, or implementation sprint.
