AI News for November 15, 2026

A November 12 lawsuit seeks an AI capability freeze, Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen, OpenAI announces the cybersecurity-focused GPT-6 Cyber, and Anthropic’s upcoming Nasdaq IPO arrives with $26 billion in anchor investment. All 77 stories from the day, below.

November 15, 202677 stories · 78 source links · 10 categories

Four nonprofits sued five AI companies on November 12, asking a California court to cap their AI capabilities at current levels; no ruling is in place. Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen as a fast, lower-cost coding and agentic model. A release tracker reports that OpenAI announced GPT-6 Cyber after it found hundreds of Linux exploits and 81 previously unknown Firefox vulnerabilities, with OpenAI classifying the model as Critical under its Preparedness Framework. One report says Microsoft filed a patent for AI that can alter spoken words and matching lip movements during live video. The new tools widen what AI systems can do while the lawsuit and live-speech patent put limits and misuse in the same frame.

Japan’s defense ministry said it will produce a Defense AI Vision by the end of November, including plans to add AI to an Air Self-Defense Force warning-and-control system by March 2027. In Indiana, candidates and experts said concerns about data centers, tax incentives, and utility costs influenced election results. At a Dublin summit, European leaders warned that AI-generated disinformation threatens upcoming EU elections. And Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Congress pressed Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick over Nvidia chip exports and Megaspeed. Defense operations, local utility bills, election integrity, and chip-export controls are becoming separate fights over public authority.

The labor picture splits depending on the time horizon. One report says more than 200,000 tech workers have lost jobs since the start of 2026—a single-source count worth treating cautiously. Meanwhile, Gartner projects AI will create 10.6 million jobs and displace 8.9 million globally between 2025 and 2030. College admissions offices are struggling to set rules as students use AI for essays and schools test AI evaluation tools. Against that uncertainty, Anthropic’s upcoming Nasdaq IPO is backed by $26 billion in anchor investment. The layoffs and institutional strain are current reports; the longer-range job gains remain projections.

What People Are Asking

What does the lawsuit to freeze AI development ask the court to do?

The complaint asks for an injunction blocking OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, and SpaceX from developing or deploying models more capable than their November 12 systems. It also seeks to prevent AI from autonomously directing further AI research and development. cointribune.com →

When is Japan’s Defense AI Vision coming out?

Japan’s Defense Minister said the new guideline is due by the end of November. It is intended to replace the ministry’s 2024 basic AI policy and prioritize practical AI deployment in defense operations and administrative work. news.yahoo.co.jp →

Is Perplexity launching AI agents on Macs?

Yes. Perplexity announced Teammates, which brings task-performing agents to Mac users for research, data analysis, and project management. voiceofemirates.com →

Could school screen time harm children’s health?

The poll does not establish that screens cause harm. It found that 47% of 2,089 UK parents worry school screen time could harm children’s health, amid clinician warnings and a government consultation on EdTech certification. britbrief.co.uk →

Are AI-led job interviews turning applicants away?

Yes, for some applicants. Survey data found seven in ten UK workers uncomfortable with AI-led interviews, while about one in ten said they were considering withdrawing their applications. yahoo.com →

Are students using AI for college applications?

Yes. Students are increasingly using AI for college essays and school selection, while universities are adopting different rules and testing AI tools for application review. mezha.net →

What are the risks of uploading photos to unfamiliar AI apps?

Haryana Police warned that uploading personal or children’s photos to unfamiliar AI apps could enable identity theft, deepfake fraud, and blackmail. uniindia.com →

Where can AI save time in a UK accounting practice?

A vendor guide says AI can save time on repeatable administrative work such as record chasing, document intake, and drafting. It emphasizes human review and confidentiality rather than unattended automation. aiagencyplus.com →
