AI News for September 21, 2026

A single report says Plugin4Shell exposed major coding assistants through malicious marketplace plugins, while British Columbia sued OpenAI over alleged warning failures. AMD crossed $1 trillion, and a separate report says SpaceXAI launched Grok 4.7. All 74 stories from the day, below.

September 21, 202674 stories · 10 categories

According to one report, a zero-click remote-code-execution flaw called Plugin4Shell affected Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot, and Gemini CLI through malicious marketplace plugins. Heise says Anthropic and OpenAI patched their tools, while Copilot remained unfixed and consumer Gemini CLI would remain vulnerable; that cross-tool account is a single-source claim worth treating cautiously. Legal exposure widened, too: British Columbia sued OpenAI and Sam Altman, alleging a failure to warn law enforcement about threatening ChatGPT messages before the Tumbler Ridge mass shooting. Euronews described a California antitrust suit accusing Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, and Google DeepMind of coordinating to slow AI development, while Verdict reported the same alleged pact with a slightly different defendant roster. The allegation is shared; the exact roster is not. Meanwhile, New York said large frontier-AI developers must register beginning in November and reach full RAISE Act compliance in January 2027.

CNBC reported that AMD rose 10% to a record high and crossed a $1 trillion market capitalization for the first time, propelled by demand for AI data-center chips. Capacity plans stretched across Asia: Aolani announced plans to deploy 22,000 Nvidia Blackwell Ultra GPUs in Malaysia and the Philippines by early 2027, and one report says Huawei moved its Ascend 960DT launch to early 2027 as DeepSeek reportedly planned to deploy at least 160,000 Huawei accelerators. Consumer hardware may be paying the opportunity cost: unconfirmed leaks suggest Nvidia and AMD could postpone their next gaming-GPU generations until 2028 while prioritizing AI data centers. A Chinese government research center also put AI penetration in key manufacturing scenarios at 34.2%, up 5.5 percentage points.

One report says SpaceXAI launched Grok 4.7 with stronger self-checking and long-context performance at unchanged base pricing. By contrast, reports of a possible Claude Opus 5.5 release remained unconfirmed by Anthropic, while Alibaba’s Qwen team reportedly released the 7-billion-parameter Qwen-Image-2.1 under a non-commercial research license. Research supplied the caveats. A 1,200-conversation study found that behavior-specific instructions reduced persona drift by 87%, but no tested intervention eliminated it. An electron-microscopy case study found silent action failures in 27.4% of records even when multimodal answers were correct. And an IBM study found employees reporting erosion of critical thinking and judgment while only 26% of organizations clearly separated human-led from AI-executed work. Better models do not remove the need to specify who—or what—is responsible for the next action.
