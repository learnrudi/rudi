AI News for October 25, 2026

Apollo’s Torsten Slok says AI has produced no financial payoff outside tech, Uber is taking ChatGPT Work from 1,000 employees to more than 30,000, Nvidia has released a local model built for a 4GB memory budget, and a UN official warns of a widening AI opportunity divide. All 54 stories from the day, below.

October 25, 202654 stories · 57 source links · 11 categories

Apollo Global Management’s Torsten Slok says AI has delivered “zero financial payoff” outside the tech industry, pointing to stagnant revenue per worker, profit margins, and productivity across the rest of the S&P 500 while the Magnificent Seven surged. Uber is expanding ChatGPT Work from a 1,000-person pilot to more than 30,000 employees, with the added oversight and coordination work still unresolved. The divide extends beyond corporate returns: a senior UN official warns that gaps in infrastructure, adoption, and language representation could widen inequality between countries. And according to one daily roundup, Texas suspended 17 data-center permits after a portal had automatically approved them.

On the product side, Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB for local assistants, game characters, and robots. A separate report based on leaked server revisions says Google is internally testing Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon, including a high-compute Max Mode; treat that as a leak, not an official announcement. AI-generated entertainment supplied the spectacle: Grok Imagine released a seven-minute Episode 1 of The Odyssey that Elon Musk reposted, while a full-length film by year-end remains an open question. Meanwhile, an India-focused roundup reported ten cybercrime, fraud, and AI-security incidents, including bank-insider fraud arrests and a cryptocurrency money-laundering network.

What People Are Asking

Has AI delivered a financial payoff outside the tech industry?

Apollo Global Management’s Torsten Slok says no. He argues that revenue per worker, profit margins, and productivity have stagnated across the rest of the S&P 500 while rising for the Magnificent Seven. Business Insider →

How many Uber employees are getting ChatGPT Work?

Uber is expanding ChatGPT Work from a 1,000-person pilot to more than 30,000 employees. The report says questions remain about whether the technology creates additional oversight and coordination work while automating tasks. tech-insider.org →

What is Nvidia’s 4GB local AI model for?

Nvidia’s Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB is a downloadable small model designed for local voice assistants, game characters, and robots within a roughly 4GB memory budget. mixed-news.com →

Has Google confirmed Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon?

Not officially, based on the available evidence. One report citing leaked Antigravity ACP server revisions says Google has begun internally testing Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon, including a high-compute Max Mode. NokiaPowerUser →

Could AI deepen global inequality?

Yes, according to a senior UN official. The warning points to unequal infrastructure, adoption, skills, and language representation as mechanisms that could widen the gap between rich and poor countries. Al Jazeera →

Did Texas suspend 17 data-center permits?

According to one daily roundup, yes: Texas suspended 17 permits after a portal automatically approved them. Treat the account as reported roundup coverage rather than independent confirmation. AI Weekly →

Is Grok making an AI version of The Odyssey?

Grok Imagine released a seven-minute AI-generated Episode 1 of The Odyssey, which Elon Musk reposted. The source raises the prospect of a full-length film by year-end but does not establish that it will be completed. Ai Miracle →

What did the October 25 cybersecurity roundup report in India?

The roundup covered ten cybercrime, fraud, and AI-security incidents, including arrests tied to bank-insider fraud, a cryptocurrency money-laundering network, and new AI-model safeguards. The420.in →
