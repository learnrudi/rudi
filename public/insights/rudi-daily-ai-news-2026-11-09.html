AI News for November 9, 2026

SHL research finds nearly 20% of U.S. workers replaced by AI were asked back, Nvidia plans to pair Korean NPUs with its GPUs, Suno faces two copyright lawsuits, and a synthetic election graphic spread a false result. All 74 stories from the day, below.

November 9, 202674 stories · 76 source links · 11 categories

SHL research found that nearly 20% of U.S. workers replaced by AI were later asked to return, often with demands for higher pay, job security, and more transparency about AI. Hiring has its own credibility problem: recruiters report that candidates are using stealth AI assistants during virtual interviews, pushing some employers toward work samples, personal questions, and in-person meetings. The workplace argument is moving from whether AI can replace people to what happens when employers discover that replacement and evaluation are harder than advertised.

Nvidia, SK Telecom, and Rebellions signed an agreement to run Korean-designed NPUs alongside Nvidia GPUs, with commercial deployment targeted for 2028. Financing and politics are pressing on the same infrastructure buildout: a funding roundup includes Mavria’s $130 million for modular AI data centers, while one weekly report argues that local ballot defeats, a chip-stock selloff, and falling AI valuations are changing the political and financial landscape for AI infrastructure. That broader conclusion comes from a single roundup and is worth treating as its author’s assessment, not a settled verdict.

The disputes over AI-generated content became unusually concrete. Suno faces separate lawsuits from Elvis Presley Enterprises and Eminem’s publisher over alleged voice cloning and training on copyrighted music. An AI-generated graphic falsely claiming Ilhan Omar won with 99% of the vote shows how synthetic media can turn a fabricated number into election-fraud fuel. Meanwhile, Rep. Don Beyer is seeking a House AI committee with subpoena power, putting oversight machinery on the congressional agenda.

What People Are Asking

Are companies rehiring workers they replaced with AI?

SHL research found that nearly 20% of U.S. workers replaced by AI were later asked to return. Many sought higher pay, job security, and transparency about how AI would be used. Fortune →

Is Nvidia going to use Korean AI chips in its data centers?

Nvidia signed an MOU with SK Telecom and Rebellions to run Korean-designed NPUs alongside Nvidia GPUs using NVLink Fusion and MGX. Commercial deployment is targeted for 2028. Seoul Economic Daily →

Is Suno being sued by the rights holders for Elvis Presley and Eminem?

Yes. Suno faces separate copyright infringement lawsuits from Elvis Presley Enterprises and Eminem’s publisher, Eight Mile Style, involving alleged voice cloning and AI training on copyrighted music. iTechPost →

Did Ilhan Omar win 99% of the vote?

No. AFP found that an AI-generated graphic falsely claimed Ilhan Omar won her district with 99% of the vote; the graphic amplified election-fraud conspiracy claims. AFP Fact Check →

Are job candidates using AI during virtual interviews?

Recruiters report that some candidates use hidden AI assistants to receive scripted answers in real time. Hiring managers are responding with personal-anecdote questions, work samples, and more in-person interviews. The Globe and Mail →

Do banks have governance in place for the AI they use?

A survey of 300 bank credit, treasury, and risk leaders across 31 countries found near-universal use of AI in decision-making, but only 18% reported fully embedded AI governance processes. Fintech Singapore →

Are Maharashtra polytechnics required to use AI tools?

The Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education directed government, aided, and unaided polytechnics to adopt tools including Google Gemini and Microsoft Copilot for teaching, learning, and administration. Compliance reports are due by January 2027. Digital Learning →

Is Congress considering a House committee focused on AI?

Rep. Don Beyer is pushing for a House Select Committee on AI with subpoena and legislative powers. The effort also includes a draft AI safety framework targeted for delivery by the end of 2026. TokenPost →
