AI News for August 26, 2026

OpenAI previews GPT-5.6, Perplexity puts a local-first agent on NVIDIA DGX Spark, data-center fights reshape midterm politics, and Brazil tightens election AI rules. All 54 stories from the day, below.

August 26, 202654 stories · 10 categories

OpenAI says its limited preview of GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna pairs stronger coding, biology, and cybersecurity capabilities with safeguards and a phased release coordinated with the U.S. government. Perplexity’s local-first Portable Computer moves some agent tasks onto small models running on NVIDIA DGX Spark, with zero token costs for on-device work. Adweek reports that Anthropic has signed no publisher licensing deals and faced no publisher lawsuits, attributing that position to the company’s fair-use stance and favorable branding. The model race is moving; the terms around it remain unsettled.

Those terms are becoming political. The Associated Press reports that opposition to data centers over water, land, and energy is reshaping midterm elections, prompting politicians to shift positions while President Trump defends the industry. In Brazil, the Superior Electoral Court has banned electoral deepfakes and required AI labels, according to the Rio Times, alongside a 72-hour blackout on synthetic content before voting days and new platform duties. Infrastructure and political speech now meet the same question: who gets to set the operating rules?

At work, experts cited by CNBC say forcing employees to use AI can be counterproductive, recommending choice, clear communication, and room to learn from mistakes. Accounting professionals may become a sign-off service for AI-generated tax and accounting work, raising questions about training, economics, and who carries liability. Physical AI adoption in Taiwanese manufacturing remains slower than expected, Deloitte reports, because talent and implementation challenges persist. One California teacher has gone further in the other direction, requiring handwriting or typewriters to reduce reliance on AI and smartphones. The common problem is operational: mandates can alienate workers, manufacturers still need talent, and AI-generated work still needs a person willing to carry liability.

Is GPT-5.6 Sol out yet?

Does Perplexity’s local AI agent have token costs?

For on-device tasks, Android Authority reports zero token costs. Perplexity’s Portable Computer uses small models including Qwen 3.8 and NVIDIA Nemotron and is available to Pro and Max users on NVIDIA DGX Spark. Android Authority →

Why hasn’t Anthropic been sued by publishers?

According to Adweek, Anthropic’s fair-use stance and favorable branding have helped it avoid publisher lawsuits despite never signing a content-licensing deal. That explanation is Adweek’s assessment of Anthropic’s position relative to its rivals. Adweek →

Are data centers affecting the midterm elections?

Yes. The Associated Press reports that opposition centered on water, land, and energy use is reshaping midterm elections, with politicians changing positions while President Trump defends the industry. Associated Press →

What are Brazil’s AI rules for the 2026 election?

The Rio Times reports that Resolution 23.755 bans electoral deepfakes, requires AI labels, creates a 72-hour blackout on synthetic content before voting days, and imposes new duties on platforms. The Rio Times →

Should companies require employees to use AI?

Experts cited by CNBC advise against mandating AI use. They recommend letting workers choose when to use it, communicating clearly, and allowing time for learning and mistakes. CNBC →

What does “liability for hire” mean in AI accounting?

It is a proposed model in which accounting professionals review and sign off on AI-generated tax and accounting work. AccountingWEB says it raises unresolved questions about liability, training, and economics. AccountingWEB →

Why is physical AI adoption slow in manufacturing?

Deloitte Taiwan reports that adoption is slower than expected because manufacturers face talent and implementation challenges despite high expectations for the technology. DigiTimes →
