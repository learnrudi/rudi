AI News for August 20, 2026

Slack brings AI coding into team channels, Nvidia routes requests to cut model costs, Congress embeds AI governance in the defense bill, and Vietnam puts AI in the national school curriculum. All 40 stories from the day, below.

August 20, 202640 stories · 10 categories

Slack launched a channel-based environment where teams and AI agents can build software with live previews and human approval. Nvidia attacked another part of the deployment problem with an open-source router reported to cut model costs by up to 74%, while adding latency and complexity, then widened its bet through data-center investment and GPU financing deals. Meanwhile, Veeda AI raised $90 million to build simulated environments for embodied-AI training. The day's infrastructure story was about control: where AI work happens, which model handles it, and who finances the machinery underneath.

The FY2026 defense bill includes secure-communications provisions, adversarial-AI restrictions, and cybersecurity oversight. Employers face a patchwork of state AI laws, including new California regulations, while Vietnam is making AI education part of the national curriculum for every public-school student. In New York City, labor leaders are pressing for governance that centers worker rights and protections. AI governance is moving out of general principles and into defense procurement, employment rules, classrooms, and collective bargaining.
