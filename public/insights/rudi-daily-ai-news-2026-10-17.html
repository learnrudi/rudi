AI News for October 17, 2026

OpenAI and Meta warn that AI deepfakes are becoming nearly impossible to verify, China proposes controls on 23 AI technologies, and KPMG reports a $170.9 billion quarter for global venture capital. Meanwhile, workers, unions, and insurers are confronting the risks created by AI monitoring and autonomous agents. All 55 stories from the day, below.

October 17, 202655 stories · 11 categories

One report says OpenAI and Meta told a federal judiciary committee that AI-generated images and video are becoming nearly impossible to verify, leaving the panel to consider new evidence rules. Experts and industry leaders are calling for government and global oversight even as rapid capability gains make coordination difficult, while China is proposing export controls on 23 AI technologies. A legal-news roundup also tracks a $400 million TikTok COPPA settlement and a California kids’ algorithm law surviving a challenge. Courts, governments, and enforcement agencies are all being pressed to decide what existing rules can still handle.

Kaiser Permanente advice nurses say AI-driven call monitoring and time metrics pressure them to shorten patient conversations, as regulators and their union push back. Separately, leaked documents reportedly suggest Amazon uses AI monitoring to surveil warehouse workers with the apparent aim of union-busting. In entertainment, AI film studios are clustering in Culver City and recruiting creative talent amid union and industry resistance. And Harbor Insurance emerged from stealth with a $10 million seed round to insure risks left by autonomous AI agents. AI risk is moving from policy language into working conditions, hiring, and insurance coverage.

KPMG’s Venture Pulse reports that global venture investment reached $170.9 billion in the third quarter, propelled by concentrated AI megadeals. An AI news roundup includes Anthropic’s C$10 billion Nova Scotia data-center lease and OpenAI’s ChatGPT Slides launch, while an infrastructure roundup highlights Microsoft’s plan to scale Maia custom silicon to multi-gigawatt deployments in 2027. Another report says China reached 34.2% AI-scenario use in manufacturing as the EU expanded a network of AI factories. The money is concentrating, and the supporting infrastructure is being planned at national and multi-gigawatt scale.
