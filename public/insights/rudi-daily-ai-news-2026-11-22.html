AI News for November 22, 2026

Microsoft is rolling out GPT-6.2 Sol worldwide as a separate report carries sharp criticism of Astra. A Character.AI licensing deal faces a privacy challenge from 39 states and D.C., Microsoft is building enterprise computing around agents, and AI video cloning is putting creators’ likenesses at risk. All 70 stories from the day, below.

November 22, 202670 stories · 72 source links · 10 categories

Microsoft is rolling out OpenAI’s GPT-6.2 Sol to eligible Copilot users worldwide, spanning Chat, Cowork, Studio, and Office apps, with usage-based billing and admin-gated access to Astra. The Daily Caller reports that developers and researchers find Astra difficult to use and uneven, even as OpenAI promotes its benchmark performance and low cost; treat the criticism as a single-outlet account. Elsewhere, a report says Alibaba pushed Qwen4-Max into enterprise cloud services while export restrictions and infrastructure costs complicate scaling. And Microsoft used Ignite to introduce an agent-centered enterprise stack, including context management, new models, Azure infrastructure, and Agent 365 governance tools.

A coalition of 39 states and D.C. is challenging Character.AI’s proposed $20 million Disney licensing deal, arguing that millions of private chats, including minors’ data, could be exposed without adequate safeguards. Germany’s top civil court suspended Renate Künast’s case against Meta and sent six platform-liability questions to the EU Court of Justice. Compute access is under pressure, too: a DEV Community post says U.S. chip restrictions now cover overseas data centers providing remote GPU access to Chinese entities, while the European Commission’s export-control forum addressed tighter rules for semiconductors, advanced computing, and sensitive research. Privacy, platform distribution, and computing capacity all came with gatekeepers attached.

A Capgemini survey found that 59% of banks and insurers are piloting or deploying multi-agent AI, but only 5% meet enterprise-wide maturity criteria. Science carries a similar gap between activity and demonstrated payoff. The Human Frontier Mission committee announced a November 24 launch built around 2,391 autonomous AI-run experiments, while Novartis says AI is speeding drug discovery and trials but that evidence of better clinical success remains years away. Outside the lab, BBC Verify reports that inexpensive AI tools are reskinning creators’ videos with generated characters, and TikTok has launched a global likeness-detection tool for public figures to find and report deepfakes. The tools are moving quickly; proof, control, and recourse are still catching up.

What People Are Asking

Is GPT-6.2 Sol available in Microsoft Copilot?

For eligible users, yes. Microsoft is rolling out GPT-6.2 Sol worldwide across Copilot Chat, Cowork, Studio, and Office apps, with usage-based billing. Wisevoter →

Why are people criticizing GPT-6.2 Astra?

The Daily Caller reports that developers and researchers have criticized Astra as difficult to use and uneven, while OpenAI promotes its benchmark performance and low cost. Treat the criticism as a single-outlet account. The Daily Caller →

Why are 39 states challenging the Character.AI-Disney deal?

A coalition of 39 states and D.C. argues that the proposed $20 million licensing deal could expose millions of private Character.AI chats, including minors’ data, without adequate privacy safeguards. The Mary Sue →

Did Germany’s top court rule that Meta is liable for recommended posts?

No final liability ruling is described. Germany’s top civil court suspended Renate Künast’s case and asked the EU Court of Justice whether algorithmic recommendations weaken Meta’s hosting protection and whether platforms must proactively detect equivalent unlawful posts. PPC Land →

Are AI agents mature enough for banks and insurers?

Mostly no, according to a Capgemini survey of 1,100 financial-services executives. Although 59% are piloting or deploying multi-agent AI, only 5% meet enterprise-wide maturity criteria, and 62% lack identity and access management suited to continuous agent execution. EUROPE SAYS →

Are AI tools cloning real content creators’ videos?

Yes. BBC Verify reports that inexpensive AI tools are being used to reskin real creators’ videos with generated characters, often to pursue adult-content revenue or viral attention. BBC News →

When does the Human Frontier Mission open?

The committee announced a global launch for November 24, 2026. It reports 2,391 autonomous AI-run experiments covering 54 challenges in 12 scientific fields, with all results scheduled for public review. AI Weekly →

Is AI already improving the clinical success of new drugs?

Not yet, based on Novartis’s account. Its executives say AI is speeding drug discovery, trial enrollment, and documentation, but evidence of improved clinical success and lower safety attrition remains years away. Markets Daily →
