The proposed bills would charge employers when AI-related job cuts occur, rather than charging AI vendors. The report describes this as a first-of-its-kind policy design. techtimes.com →

Will Singapore require teachers to take AI training?

Yes. Singapore's Ministry of Education will require primary, secondary, and junior-college teachers to complete compulsory AI modules by 2027. mustsharenews.com →

Is an AI-powered school opening in Greenwich?
