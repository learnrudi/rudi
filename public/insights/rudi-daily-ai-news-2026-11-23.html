AI News for November 23, 2026

Oregon and Virginia sue SpaceX over Grok deepfakes, Alibaba launches Qwen4-Max, a labor report finds workers without degrees face disproportionate exposure, and Marlowe raises $277 million for non-Nvidia AI infrastructure. All 74 stories from the day, below.

November 23, 202674 stories · 11 categories

Oregon and Virginia became the first states to sue SpaceX over Grok and X, alleging non-consensual sexual deepfakes, child sexual abuse material, and false safety representations. SoundCloud tightened its rules against AI slop, spam, and excessive uploads while defending its partnerships with Moises and ElevenLabs. A daily briefing says OpenAI’s chief scientist proposed industry-wide development limits. Meanwhile, one report says CrowdStrike agreed to acquire Zscaler for $43.9 billion to combine endpoint and zero-trust security for AI and agentic workloads—a single-source figure worth reading skeptically. Courts, platforms, labs, and security vendors are all being pushed to define what safer AI use actually requires.

Alibaba released Qwen4-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter sparse model with vision and a million-token context window. Google launched Gemini 4 Neon for coding, document analysis, and scientific reasoning, while Sarvam released an upgraded 105B open-weight model and defended hosting Chinese and other foreign models. The infrastructure bets are widening too: former Microsoft executives raised $277 million for Marlowe’s AMD- and Etched-based inference data centers, and a proposed Texas data center could convert bitcoin-mining power to as much as 25 MW of AI capacity if its utility, tenant, and venture conditions are met. The contest now reaches beyond model performance into chip choice, hosting, and power.

A Burning Glass Institute and RAISE US report says AI is suppressing entry-level hiring, with workers without degrees holding 80% of the jobs it identifies as most likely to be automated. Australian professional-services coverage describes AI reshaping billable hours and advisory work amid layoffs and changing graduate intakes, while a viral parody of two AI avatars interviewing each other has sharpened debate over AI screening as surveys show more than half of employers using structured AI interviews. Education systems are answering from the other direction: Seoul National University is considering a required undergraduate AI course for 2028, and Punjab plans AI training for 25,898 nominated teachers. Employers are automating parts of the first rung while schools try to make AI fluency a baseline skill.

What People Are Asking

Are Oregon and Virginia suing SpaceX over Grok deepfakes?

Yes. Oregon and Virginia became the first states to sue SpaceX over allegations that Grok and X enabled non-consensual sexual deepfakes, including alleged child sexual abuse material, and made false safety representations. CNBC →

Are workers without degrees more exposed to AI job losses?

Yes, according to a Burning Glass Institute and RAISE US report. It says workers without degrees hold 80% of the jobs most likely to be automated and are disproportionately exposed as entry-level hiring contracts. Digital Trends →

What is Alibaba’s Qwen4-Max model?

Qwen4-Max is Alibaba’s newly released sparse mixture-of-experts model with 2.4 trillion parameters, native vision, and a one-million-token context window. Alibaba claims efficiency improvements and autonomous-agent results in manufacturing and scientific research. Tech Critter →

How much does Gemini 4 Neon cost?

Google launched Gemini 4 Neon at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. It is rolling out across the Gemini app, AI Studio, Antigravity, and enterprise products. Kingy AI →

Is CrowdStrike acquiring Zscaler?

According to one report, yes. It says CrowdStrike agreed to a $43.9 billion all-stock acquisition of Zscaler to combine endpoint and zero-trust network security for enterprise AI and agentic workloads; the figure is a single-source claim worth treating cautiously. Sahyadri Startups →

Why is SoundCloud cracking down on AI uploads?

SoundCloud updated its Terms of Use and Community Guidelines to restrict AI slop, spam, and excessive uploads. The company also defended its AI partnerships with Moises and ElevenLabs. Music Business Worldwide →

What is Marlowe’s alternative to Nvidia AI infrastructure?

Marlowe is an inference-infrastructure startup founded by former Microsoft executives Dan Lewis and Kutta Srinivasan. It raised $277 million at a $1.15 billion valuation to build data centers using AMD and Etched chips instead of Nvidia GPUs. GeekWire →

Is Seoul National University making AI coursework mandatory?

Not yet. Seoul National University is considering a proposal that would make an introductory AI course a graduation requirement for every undergraduate beginning in spring 2028. Korea JoongAng Daily →
