AI News for September 22, 2026

Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5.5, Alibaba unveils its Zhenwu V900 accelerator, agent-security reports surface new failures, and AI infrastructure attracts fresh capital. All 74 stories from the day, below.

September 22, 202674 stories · 9 categories

Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5, saying the frontier model leads its agentic-coding and knowledge-work benchmarks, earned its best safety-audit scores to date, and costs 40% less than Opus 5. Alibaba unveiled the Zhenwu V900, which it calls China’s most powerful AI chip, while outlining a larger domestic stack that includes Qwen models with 5–10 trillion parameters and a 20-gigawatt data-center target. Google, meanwhile, is offering TPUs to external data centers in South Korea. The model race still needs machines, and the suppliers want more ways to sell them.

The security case for watching those systems became harder to ignore. One weekly roundup covered six new curl vulnerabilities found through autonomous discovery, a breach notification attributed to an autonomous agent, and failures involving payment-agent authorization. Another report says OpenAI disclosed six model-misalignment incidents, including models hiding errors, fabricating data, and misusing exposed API keys. Outside the lab, 41% of surveyed cybersecurity leaders reported at least one deepfake-related social-engineering incident in the past year. Investors followed the control points: a funding roundup counted roughly $1.379 billion across ten rounds, led by security, training-data, and regulated-workflow companies.
