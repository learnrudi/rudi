RUDI/About

About RUDI

Build the capacity to use AI well.

RUDI helps organizations and people prepare for, adopt, and implement AI effectively, responsibly, and humanely.

RUDI stands for

Responsible Use of Digital Intelligence.

The name is both an organizational commitment and a practical standard for how the work is done.

What RUDI is

An AI Readiness & Enablement firm.

RUDI works at two levels.

At the organizational level: strategy, workflows, technology, governance, capability, adoption, and implementation. At the individual level: understanding, judgment, skills, work design, responsibility, and agency.

Training is one mechanism—not the category.

RUDI uses training where it builds capability. But training sits inside a larger transformation that begins with readiness and connects to strategy, adoption, implementation, and measurement.

Technology is part of a human system.

RUDI examines how AI affects people, work, decisions, relationships, roles, information, institutions, and accountability—not only which features a tool provides.

Capability should remain after the engagement.

The goal is not dependence on an outside advisor. It is an organization better able to understand, decide, enable, implement, govern, and learn.

What we do

Readiness through implementation.

Five connected disciplines, held together by a human-centered, responsible, governed approach.

Leadership

Founded by Brandon Z. Hoff.

Brandon works across AI strategy, organizational adoption, education, responsible use, workflow design, and implementation. His work connects executive decisions to the capabilities people and institutions need in practice.

Founder profile

Technology, work, and institutional change.

Brandon is an applied AI practitioner, educator, and social entrepreneur trained in finance. His work began with machine-learning models for predictive lending and now spans enterprise adoption, workforce enablement, public-interest technology, real estate and the built environment, speaking, and community leadership.

He created Hoff Digital, a public education community on responsible AI that has reached nearly 100,000 followers, and developed curriculum with The Propel Center, the University of Illinois Springfield, and public, private, and civic institutions. His training includes an MBA in Finance and Social Entrepreneurship from EDHEC, IBM AI Developer coursework, DeepLearning.AI, and MIT xPRO.
