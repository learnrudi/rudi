RUDI/About

Responsible Use of Digital Intelligence

Help your team put AI to work.

RUDI helps organizations train their people, improve everyday workflows, and build AI tools with clear responsibility for how they are used.

Based in Cincinnati and serving organizations nationally, we connect hands-on education with product development and ongoing support.

Meet the founder

Brandon Z. Hoff

Founder, educator &
AI product engineer

Brandon develops AI products and teaches people how to use them. His work spans machine-learning models for predictive lending, enterprise AI adoption, and curriculum development with The Propel Center and the University of Illinois Springfield.

He created Hoff Digital to teach responsible AI use. His education includes an MBA in Finance and Social Entrepreneurship from EDHEC, with further training through IBM AI Developer coursework, DeepLearning.AI, and MIT xPRO.
