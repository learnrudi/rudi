About RUDI

Build the capacity to use AI well.

RUDI helps organizations and people prepare for, adopt, and implement AI effectively, responsibly, and humanely.

RUDI stands for

Responsible Use of Digital Intelligence.

The name is both an organizational commitment and a practical standard for how the work is done.

What RUDI is

An AI Readiness & Enablement firm.

RUDI works at two levels.

At the organizational level: strategy, workflows, technology, governance, capability, adoption, and implementation. At the individual level: understanding, judgment, skills, work design, responsibility, and agency.

Training connects to assessment and implementation.

We use assessment findings to shape training, help teams apply what they learn, and measure how their use of AI changes.

We examine how AI changes people’s work.

We look at the tasks AI could support, the people affected, and who remains responsible for decisions and results.

Your team keeps the skills and working practices.

We help leaders and teams learn to evaluate AI, manage workflows, review results, and decide when to change their approach.

Leadership

Founded by Brandon Z. Hoff.

Brandon works across product development, AI engineering, organizational adoption, and education. His work connects executive decisions to the capabilities people and institutions need in practice.

Founder profile

Product engineering and AI education.

Brandon is the founder of RUDI, an AI product engineer, and an educator. He develops AI products and helps organizations build the skills and practices to use them well. His work began with machine-learning models for predictive lending and now spans enterprise adoption, workforce enablement, public-interest technology, real estate and the built environment, speaking, and community leadership.

He created Hoff Digital, a public education community on responsible AI that has reached nearly 100,000 followers, and developed curriculum with The Propel Center, the University of Illinois Springfield, and public, private, and civic institutions. His training includes an MBA in Finance and Social Entrepreneurship from EDHEC, IBM AI Developer coursework, DeepLearning.AI, and MIT xPRO.
