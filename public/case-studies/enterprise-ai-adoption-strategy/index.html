RUDI/Case Studies/Enterprise AI Adoption Strategy

Engagement Profile · Logistics & Supply Chain

Enterprise AI adoption strategy for a 1,200-employee organization.

Walker SCM engaged RUDI for corporate-wide AI adoption strategy and rollout planning focused on workforce productivity and responsible adoption.

Evidence note

This page documents publicly stated engagement scope.

Quantitative client outcomes have not been published and are not inferred here.

OrganizationWalker SCM
SectorLogistics / Supply Chain
Scale1,200 employees
RUDI roleStrategy and rollout planning

The organizational challenge

Move from individual AI interest to an enterprise adoption model.

For a workforce of 1,200, rollout planning has to account for different roles, training needs, and access to systems. Leaders need shared priorities and clear guidance on how employees should use AI.

The engagement focused on the organization-wide strategy and planning required to support that transition.

RUDI’s role

Corporate-wide strategy and rollout planning.

Public descriptions of the engagement identify two primary objectives: workforce productivity and responsible adoption.

01

Adoption strategy

Plan how AI use affects employee responsibilities, skills, and everyday workflows.

02

Rollout planning

Consider how an enterprise introduces, communicates, enables, supports, and governs use across a varied workforce.

03

Responsible productivity

Connect productivity opportunities to appropriate judgment, organizational safeguards, and sustainable employee practice.

Why the engagement matters

What a rollout across 1,200 employees needs to address.

Leadership

Direction

A corporate-wide initiative needs shared priorities, sponsorship, expectations, and decision criteria.

Workforce

Capability

A 1,200-person organization contains different roles, workflows, starting points, needs, and adoption barriers.

Governance

Trust

Responsible adoption requires boundaries, usable guidance, human oversight, and organizational accountability.

Rollout

Sequence

Communication, enablement, access, support, workflow application, measurement, and learning must reinforce one another.
