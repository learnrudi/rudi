RUDI · Live AI Workflow Clinic

Build an AI workflow for a task you repeat.

A small-group Zoom-style clinic. Cameras are optional; participation is encouraged. Bring one recurring task and leave with a workflow you can reuse, review, and hand off.

What you leave with

One recurring task turned into an AI-assisted workflow.

Bring one repeated piece of knowledge work to an interactive room. You will see selected workflows diagnosed live, ask practical questions, and leave with a mapped workflow, reusable instructions, review criteria, and a clear decision on whether it belongs in chat, a project, reusable instructions, or a connector-enabled workflow.

01

Map the work

Clarify the goal, inputs, context, constraints, handoff points, and output you need from an AI tool.

02

Choose the right surface

Decide what belongs in a chat, a project, reusable instructions, or a connector-backed workflow.

03

Leave with reusable instructions

Use a practical template for instructions, examples, review criteria, and follow-up improvements.

01

Workflow map

A concrete map of one recurring task: inputs, context, steps, output, review, and owner.

02

AI setup checklist

A practical checklist for when to use chat, projects, reusable instructions, connectors, or a simpler process.

03

Reusable instruction template

A fill-in structure for role, task, context, examples, constraints, and quality standards.

04

QA/review checklist

A review pass for accuracy, privacy, tone, source checking, and human approval points.

05

Guided intake form

A pre-session intake that helps identify useful work even if you do not know the problem yet.

06

7-day replay

Replay access so you can revisit the examples and refine the workflow after the clinic.

Who it is for

People repeating knowledge work with AI.

This is for team leads, operators, consultants, educators, founders, and builders who already know AI can help, but need better judgment about how to structure the work. Each RUDI clinic is an interactive small-group session built around one focused workflow theme.
