AI won't fix a broken workflow.

What you leave with

One recurring task turned into an AI-assisted workflow.

Bring one repeated piece of knowledge work to an interactive room. You will see selected workflows diagnosed live, ask practical questions, and leave with a mapped workflow, reusable instructions, review criteria, and a clear decision on whether it belongs in chat, a project, reusable instructions, or a connector-enabled workflow.

Replay access so you can revisit the examples and refine the workflow after the clinic.

Who it is for

People repeating knowledge work with AI.

This is for team leads, operators, consultants, educators, founders, and builders who already know AI can help, but need better judgment about how to structure the work. Each RUDI clinic is an interactive small-group session built around one focused workflow theme.
