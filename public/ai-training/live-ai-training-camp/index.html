Before You Submit A Work Example

Keep the work useful and safe.

Will every example be coached live?

No. Selected examples may be worked through during the Campfire. Priority goes to examples that are specific, useful, and safe for the room to learn from.

Can I stay anonymous?

Yes. You can submit a work example anonymously or ask that your name not be shown if it is selected.

What should I avoid sharing?

Do not submit confidential client material, private health information, sensitive legal facts, financial account data, passwords, API keys, or proprietary documents you cannot discuss publicly.

Is this personal consulting?

No. It is a live training session with selected examples. The goal is to teach through real work, not provide unlimited one-on-one consulting.
